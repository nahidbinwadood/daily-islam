# Ramadan spikes, prayer times never stop

Research date: 5 November 2026. This report maps how Islamic search demand in Bangladesh moves across the Islamic and Gregorian year, the week and the day. It gives 2026 dates verified for Bangladesh and 2027 planning dates, and it sets out what the timing implies for serving users all year round. Query-level detail is in [03-bangladesh-keyword-research.md](03-bangladesh-keyword-research.md), user journeys in [04-user-search-intent-and-behavior.md](04-user-search-intent-and-behavior.md), the related gaps (occasion engine, BD Hijri date) in [07-content-and-feature-gaps.md](07-content-and-feature-gaps.md), and Bangladesh-specific requirements in [06-bangladesh-specific-user-needs.md](06-bangladesh-specific-user-needs.md). Methods are in [10-research-methodology-and-sources.md](10-research-methodology-and-sources.md), and row-level data in [data/keyword-database.csv](data/keyword-database.csv).

## Key findings

- **Ramadan week 1 is the year's biggest wave.** "রমজান" (Ramadan) runs about **40x its off-season level** in the week Ramadan starts (82 vs about 2 in 2026). More than ten Ramadan terms spike together for four weeks, then collapse within a week of Eid. Demand is front-loaded: the first week is always the peak.
- **Shab-e-Barat is the biggest one-night occasion, not the biggest event of the year.** Per term, its peak (6.3x the prayer-time median week) is level with Ramadan week-1 terms (6.1–7.1x) and well below "ঈদ" (16.7x) or "দোয়া" (11.1x) at their peaks. Separately, prayer-time searches hit their yearly high in Shab-e-Barat week in 2022, 2023 and 2026. This corrects earlier research (Nov 4 2026), which called Shab-e-Barat "the single biggest religious spike of the year".
- **Every other occasion is a sharp one-week spike from near zero.** Measured peaks against the prayer-time median week are Shab-e-Meraj 3.1x, Eid-e-Milad-un-Nabi 1.6x, Laylatul Qadr 1.5x, Eid-ul-Adha 1.5x, Hajj (Latin) 1.4x, Ashura 1.05x, and Bishwa Ijtema, Qurbani and Arafah about 0.8x. Most seasonal terms are zero in 65–91% of weeks. Searches start only 1–3 weeks before the event and vanish the week after.
- **The weekly cycle is as strong as the annual one for Friday content.** On Fridays "জুমার নামাজ" (Jumu'ah prayer) runs about 40–60x and "সূরা কাহাফ" (Surah Kahf) about 25–45x their other-weekday levels. Prayer-time searches rise 8–20% on Fridays.
- **A small set of needs never switches off.** Prayer times, dua, Quran, hadith, Islamic names and umrah are searched in every week of five years, with peaks only 1.8–2.8x their median (dua 5.1x). Dua alone averages 2.5x the prayer-time median.
- **Site traffic moves far less than keywords.** Ahrefs' traffic estimates for hadithbd.com and muslimbangla.com show a Ramadan-month high of about **1.2–1.9x** the previous month, against 5–40x for individual Ramadan keywords. Most of their traffic is year-round.
- **People plan months ahead and want Bangladesh's date.** On 5 Nov 2026, autocomplete already offered 2027 dates for Ramadan, Shab-e-Barat, Eid, Hajj and Ijtema, usually with "বাংলাদেশ" appended. In 2027, Ijtema, Shab-e-Meraj, Shab-e-Barat and the start of Ramadan all fall between early January and mid-February.

## How to read the magnitudes

All magnitudes come from Google Trends for Bangladesh, pulled live on 5 Nov 2026 through the Trends widget API: 5-year weekly data (31 Oct 2021 – 7 Nov 2026) and 90 days of daily data. Every request included the anchor **"নামাজের সময়" (prayer time)**. Each term is expressed as a multiple of the anchor's 5-year **median week** ("x anchor-median"), so terms from different requests can be compared ([Trends T2](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%205-y&geo=BD&q=%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%9C%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B0%20%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%AF%E0%A6%BC,%E0%A6%B6%E0%A6%AC%E0%A7%87%20%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%A4,%E0%A6%B6%E0%A6%AC%E0%A7%87%20%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%A6%E0%A6%B0,%E0%A6%B6%E0%A6%AC%E0%A7%87%20%E0%A6%AE%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%9C,ramadan%20calendar)). These are **relative indices, not search counts**. Trends rounds to integers, so terms with values under about 5 are imprecise. It matches exact phrases, so spelling matters: "সূরা কাহাফ" is about 40x "সূরা কাহফ" on Fridays. "ঈদ" is a broad term that includes holidays, films and entertainment. Weekly buckets run Sunday to Saturday. **[Measured]** marks Trends and Ahrefs figures. **[Observed]** marks autocomplete snapshots taken on 5 Nov 2026, which show what people type, not how much. **[Inferred]** marks the analyst's interpretation.

The 2026 dates check out against the data. **Every verified 2026 date falls inside the Trends peak week for its term**: Meraj 11–17 Jan, Barat 1–7 Feb, Ramadan 15–21 Feb, Qadr and Eid-ul-Fitr 15–21 Mar, Qurbani 24–30 May, Ashura 21–27 Jun, Milad 23–29 Aug. Bangladesh celebrated **both Eids of 2026 one day after Saudi Arabia**, and bdnews24 describes this as the traditional pattern ([bdnews24](https://bdnews24.com/bangladesh/8618af4cfd38)). The 2026 Ramadan start date comes from a search-result summary that was not opened. It is consistent with the Trends week-1 peak.

**2027 dates are planning estimates (±1 day, subject to moon sighting), except two.** Ijtema (8–10 and 15–17 Jan, announced by the Home Minister on 3 Sep 2026) and the Hajj roadmap (Ministry of Religious Affairs, published 7 Jun 2026) are official. No official 2027 holiday list had been found by 5 Nov 2026. The 2026 list was published on 10 Nov 2025, so the 2027 list may be imminent ([Daily Star Bangla](https://bangla.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/news-712741)). The 2027 Ramadan start is the date most in doubt. The seasonal researcher counts back to about 9–10 Feb from a 10–11 Mar Eid, while the earlier market research cited Wego's projection of about 8 Feb. Both are estimates. Content should be ready a week before the earlier one.

## Inside Ramadan: week 1 for times, the last ten days for fitra

Ramadan is the largest **cluster**: more than ten terms spike together and stay high for four weeks. In every year from 2022 to 2026 the peak was **the week Ramadan started** (27 Mar–2 Apr 2022; 19–25 Mar 2023; 10–16 Mar 2024; 23 Feb–1 Mar 2025; 15–21 Feb 2026) ([Trends T1](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%205-y&geo=BD&q=%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%9C%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B0%20%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%AF%E0%A6%BC,%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%9C%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%A8,%E0%A6%B8%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B9%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%BF,%E0%A6%87%E0%A6%AB%E0%A6%A4%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B0,%E0%A6%B8%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B9%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%B0%20%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%AF%E0%A6%BC)). [Measured] In 2026 "রমজান" scored **82 in week 1**, then 28, 23, 25 and 20, and fell to 2 within a week of Eid. Its off-season level (Sep–Nov 2026) is about 2, which gives the **roughly 40x** peak-to-off-season ratio. "সেহরির সময়" (sehri time) scored 66 in week 1 against 1–2 off-season. Its 5-year maximum, 100, came in 19–25 Mar 2023. "ইফতার" decays more slowly than "সেহরি" (2026: 68 → 41 → 33 → 29 → 22, against 58 → 17 → 11 → 10 → 6). [Inferred] Sehri searching drops quickly once the habit is set, while iftar searching continues through the month.

Within the month, needs arrive in sequence. Zakat peaks in weeks 1–3 (15/14/14 in 2026), fitra climbs through Ramadan to peak in the last ten days (1 → 17 → 27 → 42 → 44), and Laylatul Qadr peaks in the 27th-night week ([Trends T5](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%205-y&geo=BD&q=%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%9C%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B0%20%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%AF%E0%A6%BC,%E0%A6%93%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B9,%E0%A6%95%E0%A7%81%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%BF,%E0%A6%AB%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%A4%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%BE,%E0%A6%97%E0%A6%B0%E0%A7%81%E0%A6%B0%20%E0%A6%B9%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%9F); [Trends T7](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%205-y&geo=BD&q=%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%9C%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B0%20%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%AF%E0%A6%BC,%E0%A6%B0%E0%A7%8B%E0%A6%9C%E0%A6%BE,%E0%A6%A4%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%B0%20%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%9C,%E0%A6%AF%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%A4,%E0%A6%B2%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%87%E0%A6%B2%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%A4%E0%A7%81%E0%A6%B2%20%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%A6%E0%A6%B0)). The week of 15–21 Mar 2026, which covered the last nights, Laylatul Qadr and Eid, was the peak for how-to and ruling terms such as নামাজের নিয়ম, জায়েজ, কাজা নামাজ and শবে কদর ([Trends BD 12-m](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?geo=BD&date=today%2012-m)). ## Site traffic rises only 1.2–1.9x in Ramadan

Keyword spikes of 5–40x do not translate into site-level spikes of the same size. Ahrefs' **modelled** monthly organic-traffic estimates for the two BD-focused sites show a Ramadan or post-Ramadan monthly high of about **1.2–1.9x the previous month**, followed by a drop ([Ahrefs hadithbd.com](https://ahrefs.com/websites/hadithbd.com); [Ahrefs muslimbangla.com](https://ahrefs.com/websites/muslimbangla.com)). ## Serving users year-round: a daily, weekly and annual rhythm

The following is **[Inferred]** from the measured patterns above.

**Daily: prayer times plus one small item.** Prayer times and dua are the only Islamic searches that never stop, and both peak around Fajr. A fast "today" view for the user's district, with the next prayer, sehri and iftar, and the day's Hijri date (BD moon sighting), paired with one dua or a few ayat with উচ্চারণ and audio, matches the strongest daily demand. The second daily window is Asr to Maghrib, which fits evening adhkar. Dream interpretation peaks in the same morning window. It is an engagement opportunity, but it needs careful framing (see [07](07-content-and-feature-gaps.md)).

**Weekly: Friday.** Surah Kahf, Jumu'ah rakat and niyat, the khutbah text (users search for "প্রথম ও দ্বিতীয় খুতবা pdf / বাংলা উচ্চারণ", the first and second khutbah) and shareable Friday greetings. Friday multiplies these searches 25–60x, so it is the most reliable recurring traffic moment outside Ramadan.

**Annual: an occasion engine.** About 10 occasion pages, each moving from countdown and BD date, to how-to, to next year's date. Each goes live 2–3 weeks before the event, because ramps are short (Shab-e-Barat and Qurbani build over 2–4 weeks; Milad and Ashura over 1–2 weeks). In 2026 the occasion months were January (Meraj; Ijtema in normal years), February (Barat, Ramadan), March (Qadr, Eid), May (Hajj, Qurbani), June (Ashura) and August/September (Milad). That gives users a reason to return roughly every one to two months.

**The quiet season is September to December.** After Milad there is no major occasion until Ijtema and Meraj in January. Year-round content (Quran, hadith, names, dua) and life-event searches (births and aqiqah, death rites, marriage) must carry engagement in that period. It is also the build window before the 2027 cluster.

**Q1 2027 concentrates the year's seasonal demand.** In 2027, Ijtema (8–10 and 15–17 Jan), Shab-e-Meraj (about 5–6 Jan), Shab-e-Barat (about 23 Jan) and the start of Ramadan (about 8–10 Feb) all fall within about six weeks, followed by Laylatul Qadr (about 6 Mar) and Eid-ul-Fitr (about 10–11 Mar). A product that is not live and indexed by **mid-December 2026** will miss the reactivation moment (Shab-e-Barat) and the acquisition moment (Ramadan week 1). Reviews show Ramadan is when many users adopt an Islamic app ("রমজান মাসের ওসিলায়", on the occasion of Ramadan) ([Play: Quran Majeed](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pakdata.QuranMajeed&hl=bn&gl=BD)).

**Acquisition and retention moments differ.** Ramadan week 1 brings the most new users, searching for sehri and iftar times. Shab-e-Barat week, when prayer-time searches hit their yearly high, brings lapsed users back. Friday and the daily prayer-time view are what keep them between occasions.

## Confidence and limitations

Confidence is **high** in the **shape** of the seasonal pattern: which occasions spike, in which week, and how short the ramps are. Five years of weekly data agree with every verified 2026 date and with an independent 2018 report. Confidence is **medium** in cross-term magnitudes. Trends rounds small values to integers, exact-phrase matching undercounts terms with several spellings (হজ/হজ্জ, ওমরাহ/ওমরা), and "ঈদ" is a broad, partly non-religious term. Confidence is **low** in absolute volumes and site traffic. Trends gives relative indices only, and the site figures are Ahrefs and Semrush model estimates that conflict with each other. No first-party or Similarweb traffic history was available, and Similarweb blocked automated access. The Hajj and Umrah registration and package terms are too small in Trends to resolve. Their seasonality rests on autocomplete and news, and the reported 24 Sep registration deadline and the government package price of 5,05,648 Tk come from search snippets and are unverified ([Jugantor](https://www.jugantor.com/national/1154128)). **All 2027 Islamic dates except Ijtema and the Hajj roadmap are estimates**, and the 2027 Ramadan start differs by one to two days between sources. The Shab-e-Meraj 2027 date is the analyst's own calculation with no external source. Autocomplete is a single off-season snapshot, so event-week phrasing ("আজকের", today's) is under-represented. Hourly data comes from earlier research and covers no Ramadan week. No app-store seasonality data for Bangladesh was found. Related-query growth figures for occasions ("কদরের রাতের দোয়া" +250%, "আশুরার দিনের আমল" +110%) come from earlier research (Nov 4 2026) and were not re-verified. See [10-research-methodology-and-sources.md](10-research-methodology-and-sources.md).

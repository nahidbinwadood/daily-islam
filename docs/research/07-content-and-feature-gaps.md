# Trust, Bangla search and BD dates

Research date: 5 November 2026. This report turns the demand evidence in [03-bangladesh-keyword-research.md](03-bangladesh-keyword-research.md) and the user evidence in [04-user-search-intent-and-behavior.md](04-user-search-intent-and-behavior.md) into a ranked list of content and feature gaps. For each gap it shows what exists today, what is missing, and how much demand sits behind it. Existing solutions are summarised from the site audit in [05-competitor-feature-analysis.md](05-competitor-feature-analysis.md) and [02-top-islamic-websites-bangladesh.md](02-top-islamic-websites-bangladesh.md). Bangladesh-specific requirements are detailed in [06-bangladesh-specific-user-needs.md](06-bangladesh-specific-user-needs.md), and timing in [08-seasonal-search-trends.md](08-seasonal-search-trends.md). Row-level keyword data: [data/keyword-database.csv](data/keyword-database.csv).

## Key findings

- **The largest gaps are about trust and local fit, not missing topics.** Bangladeshi users can already find almost every topic somewhere. What they rarely find is search that understands how they spell, Bangla-script pronunciation, a cited grade on every hadith and dua, dates that follow Bangladesh's moon sighting, and answers labelled by madhhab.
- **Bangla-aware search ranks first.** quran.com's search API returns 0 results for the everyday word "নামাজ" (it indexes "সালাত"). Only hadithbd handles Banglish ("namaj"), and none of the BD sites tested corrects a typo such as "নামজ". The demand is spread across spelling variants that differ by 6–300x.
- **Topic hadith is the clearest content gap.** Queries such as "মা নিয়ে হাদিস" (hadith about mothers) and short-hadith lists draw >1,000 searches a month in Ahrefs' estimate, yet caption and quote sites win them with no Arabic text, no grading and heavy ad scripts.
- **Bangladesh-specific utilities are weak or absent.** No site audited cites the BD National Moon Sighting Committee for its Hijri date, even though Bangladesh celebrated both Eids of 2026 a day after Saudi Arabia. Only one prayer-time site shows both Hanafi and Shafi'i Asr times. Zakat and faraid calculators that rank are mostly foreign, charity-run or thin.
- **Fact-checking viral claims is unclaimed.** Queries for "৮০ বছরের গুনাহ মাফের দোয়া" (a dua that forgives 80 years of sins) and "জুমার নামাজ ২২ রাকাত" (22 rakat of Jumu'ah) show people looking up popular claims, and no site addresses them. Search volume is small, but credibility value is high.
- **Some apparent gaps are thin.** A Bangla tasbih counter has no Bangla competitor, but measured demand is tiny (Ahrefs >100, KD Hard; Trends 0.003x). Hifz tools and licensed book PDFs matter to a niche. They rank low on demand and high on complexity.

## How the opportunities were scored

Each opportunity gets five 1–5 scores. **Demand** comes from Ahrefs buckets and Trends ratios where they exist, and from observed queries otherwise: 5 means a head term at or above 0.10x the prayer-time anchor or many >10,000 items, and 1 means below 0.01x or Ahrefs <100. **Severity** is how badly the current results fail the user, judged from SERP observations and reviews: 5 means misinformation, harm or broken worship. **Competitive gap** is how far existing BD sites are from meeting the need: 5 means nobody does it. **Implementation complexity** runs from 1 (easy) to 5 (hard), counting editorial, scholarly and data effort as well as engineering. **Long-term value** is the contribution to trust, retention and defensibility. The priority score is **Demand + Severity + Competitive gap + Long-term value + (6 − Complexity)**, out of 25. Ties are broken by Demand, then Long-term value. **All scores are analyst judgements based on the cited evidence, not measurements.** Another analyst weighting, for example, demand double, would reorder the middle of the list. The top five are robust to reasonable re-weighting.

## Where the gaps cluster: trust, local fit and Bangla language

Read top to bottom, the ranking is less a list of features than **three structural gaps**. The first is **trust at the item level**: grading, sources, named translators, corrections and myth-checks (rows 4, 6, 7, 9, 12, 16). Bangladeshi users already show this need. They type "আল কাউসার" and "শায়খ আহমাদুল্লাহ" into queries, praise "সনদ সহীহ" (sahih chains) in reviews and report wrong words verse by verse ([Play: IRD Dua](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ihadis.dua&hl=bn&gl=BD)). Yet the audit found that the two most-used BD sites, muslimbangla and ihadis, do not name translators or graders, and muslimbangla's duas carry no references ([05](05-competitor-feature-analysis.md); [muslimbangla dua](https://muslimbangla.com/dua-category/1)). Closing this gap is mostly editorial work and metadata, which is why its complexity scores are low.

The second gap is **Bangladesh-specific correctness**: moon-sighting Hijri dates, IF-matched district and upazila times, both Asr times, BD-unit zakat and BD-law faraid (rows 3, 5, 10, 19, 20). The government sources are effectively absent as products. The Islamic Foundation portal has no prayer times or Hijri calendar, and quran.gov.bd still serves verse images built in 2012 ([IF](https://islamicfoundation.gov.bd/); [quran.gov.bd](https://quran.gov.bd/)). That leaves third parties to claim "Islamic Foundation timing" with no way to check it. One conflict here needs resolving before building. A 2026 Ittefaq report says other districts derive times by adding or subtracting up to 9 minutes from Dhaka ([Ittefaq](https://www.ittefaq.com.bd/775700/%E0%A6%9A%E0%A6%B2%E0%A6%A4%E0%A6%BF-%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%9B%E0%A6%B0%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B0-%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%9C%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%A8%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B0-%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B9%E0%A7%8D%E2%80%8C%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%BF-%E0%A6%93-%E0%A6%87%E0%A6%AB%E0%A6%A4%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B0%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B0-%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%AE%E0%A7%9F%E0%A6%B8%E0%A7%82%E0%A6%9A%E0%A6%BF-%E0%A6%86%E0%A6%9C)). A Jugantor snippet quotes the IF calling that method "ভুল ও ভিত্তিহীন" (wrong and baseless), because the IF now publishes a schedule for every district ([Jugantor](https://www.jugantor.com/islam-life/1057413)). The IF's actual method could not be verified.

The third gap is **Bangla as users actually type it**: search, pronunciation and spelling-tolerant matching (rows 1, 2). This is the hardest to copy, because it compounds. Every new page becomes reachable by more spellings. It is also the only gap that improves every other row.

Two low-ranked items deserve a note. The **tasbih counter** looks like an open field, since no Bangla tool ranks, but Ahrefs rates "tasbih counter" KD Hard at only >100 searches, and most counter queries are for buying a device ([Ahrefs tasbih counter](https://ahrefs.com/keyword-generator/?country=bd&input=tasbih%20counter)). It is worth building only as a cheap attachment to zikir pages, not as a destination. **Hifz** shows a conflict between two of our observations. The site audit found a hifz tracker on hadithbd, but the Bing results for "কুরআন হিফজ করার সহজ উপায়" (an easy way to memorise the Quran) show only articles and videos ([hadithbd](https://www.hadithbd.com/); [Bing](https://www.bing.com/search?q=%E0%A6%95%E0%A7%81%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%86%E0%A6%A8%20%E0%A6%B9%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%AB%E0%A6%9C%20%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B0%20%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%B9%E0%A6%9C%20%E0%A6%89%E0%A6%AA%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%AF%E0%A6%BC&cc=BD&setlang=bn)). The tool exists, but it is not discoverable through search.

## What is deliberately not a gap

**Ad-free pages are table stakes among dedicated Islamic sites, not a differentiator.** No ads were seen on hadithbd, ihadis, muslimbangla, quran.com, islamqa or alkawsar. Ads appeared only on newspaper religion sections such as Jugantor and Prothom Alo ([Jugantor](https://www.jugantor.com/islam-life); [Prothom Alo](https://www.prothomalo.com/religion)). Ads still matter as a **leave-reason**: they are the largest complaint theme in the review corpus, with 551 matches ([Play: Muslim Pro](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bitsmedia.android.muslimpro&hl=bn&gl=BD)). The implication is that going ad-free avoids a penalty but earns no advantage against the serious Bangla sites. Likewise, **library-style hadith browsing** (Bukhari and Muslim by number) is well served by hadithbd and ihadis, and Google demand for it is small ([Bing সহিহ বুখারী বাংলা](https://www.bing.com/search?q=%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%B9%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%B9%20%E0%A6%AC%E0%A7%81%E0%A6%96%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B0%E0%A7%80%20%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%82%E0%A6%B2%E0%A6%BE&cc=BD&setlang=bn)). Building a better hadith library is not where the gap is. Attaching grades and references to topic pages that link into a library is.

## Confidence and limitations

The **gap observations** (who ranks, what features exist) are moderately reliable. They come from single-day snapshots: 6 Google SERPs before a CAPTCHA, 34 Google SERPs in a signed-in browser, 16 Bing SERPs, and site visits through curl and a headless browser from outside Bangladesh. "Not found" in the audit means not seen in the navigation or on the pages examined, not proven absent ([05](05-competitor-feature-analysis.md)). Native apps were not audited, and some features may live only there. The **demand scores** inherit the limits in [03](03-bangladesh-keyword-research.md). There are no absolute volumes outside Quran, hadith and dua, Trends is relative and exact-phrase only, and autocomplete is not volume. The **severity, gap, complexity and value scores are analyst judgement**. They are reasoned from the evidence cited but not measured, and the priority formula weights the five dimensions equally by choice. No page-by-page accuracy grading was done. "Poor result" judgements rest on automated checks (Arabic character counts, ad-script references, reference counts) and on the type of domain ranking. Facebook, YouTube and TikTok, where captions, women's questions and ruqyah demand concentrate, were not measured. Complexity estimates leave out licensing negotiations for translations, tafsir and audio, which could dominate the real cost; see [01](01-bangladesh-islamic-digital-market.md) for market context. See [10-research-methodology-and-sources.md](10-research-methodology-and-sources.md).

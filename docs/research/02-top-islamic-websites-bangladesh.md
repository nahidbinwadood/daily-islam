# Two Bangla portals lead Bangladesh's Islamic web

**Key findings**

- **muslimbangla.com and hadithbd.com form a clear first tier.** They draw an estimated **~905K and ~601K Bangladesh visits a month** respectively (Similarweb, September 2026), with Bangladesh ranks **#460 and #478**. Every other Bangla-native Islamic site is at least **3× smaller**.
- **The second tier draws ~100–360K Bangladesh visits a month.** It contains salat.habibur.com (prayer times, desktop-only data), alqurans.com (Bangla Quran), alkawsar.com (Hanafi monthly and fatwa archive) and ihadis.com (IRD hadith).
- **Global giants have unknown Bangladesh volume.** Bangladesh is outside the top-5 countries of quran.com (19.7M global visits) and islamqa.info (8.3M), so only **upper bounds** exist: below ~1.14M and below ~403K Bangladesh visits respectively. sunnah.com's Bangladesh share is **unavailable**.
- **The tools disagree on absolute volumes by roughly 2–3×.** Rank order is the reliable output. One example: Ahrefs' organic-only estimate for ihadis.com (405K) is *larger* than Similarweb's all-channel total (206.7K).
- **Search is the engine.** Organic search is the #1 channel (48–66% of desktop visits) for 7 of the 9 Bangla sites whose top channel was visible. The head queries are **prayer times**, for example নামাজের সময়সূচি ("prayer schedule", 37K/month, Ahrefs BD), followed by duas, single surahs, baby names and seasonal worship.
- **Newspapers dwarf all Islamic sites.** prothomalo.com has ~20.0M implied Bangladesh visits and is Bangladesh's **#10** site, but its religion-section traffic is **unmeasurable** with free tools.
- **Several obvious Bangla Islamic domains are defunct.** islamijibon.com and quranbangla.com are for sale, and al-hadith.com is parked. salat.habibur.com itself points users to a newer site.

## Methodology: modelled estimates, one ambiguous label, and three tools that disagree

**All traffic numbers here are third-party model estimates, not first-party analytics.** No site owner publishes analytics.

The primary source is **Similarweb's free website pages**, showing September 2026 data, fetched 2026-11-05 ([Similarweb muslimbangla.com](https://www.similarweb.com/website/muslimbangla.com/)). Two limitations apply:

- **The headline "Total Visits" figure sits beside a "Total Visits Last 3 Months" chart**, so it could be a three-month total or a single month. We treat it as the **latest single month**, for two reasons. That is Similarweb's standard free-page layout. And for quran.com, 19.7M as one month fits Ahrefs' 6.3M organic-only estimate combined with quran.com's 46.9% direct share. **If the figure is actually a three-month total, divide every implied Bangladesh figure below by 3.** The ranking order would not change.
- **The traffic-source split is limited.** Similarweb's free page shows only the top three channels, gives a percentage only for the first, and shows desktop only. Top pages are paywalled.

**"Implied BD visits" = global visits × Bangladesh share**, both taken from the same tool and month. Every implied figure is therefore an **estimate of an estimate**.

The cross-check is **Ahrefs' free pages** (ahrefstop.com, October 2026). Ahrefs measures **organic search traffic only**, so its figures are not comparable to Similarweb's all-channel totals ([Ahrefs muslimbangla.com](https://ahrefstop.com/websites/muslimbangla.com)). Its per-keyword Bangladesh volumes, such as 37K for নামাজের সময়সূচি, are model outputs. They are more granular than the volume buckets Ahrefs' free keyword generator returns (see [report 10](10-research-methodology-and-sources.md)).

## Newspapers as context, not competitors we can measure

prothomalo.com recorded **30.4M visits** in September 2026 with a **65.82% Bangladesh share (~20.0M implied Bangladesh visits)**, ranking **#10 in Bangladesh**. Direct traffic leads at 69.47%, and none of its visible top keywords are religious ([Similarweb prothomalo.com](https://www.similarweb.com/website/prothomalo.com/)). Section-level traffic for Prothom Alo ধর্ম (religion), Jugantor ইসলাম ও জীবন (Islam and life), Kaler Kantho ইসলাম, Dhaka Post ইসলাম or Jago News ধর্ম would need paid subfolder reports. The domain totals for jugantor, kalerkantho, dhakapost and jagonews24 were not fetched.

As a hypothetical, **1–3% of Prothom Alo's Bangladesh traffic would be 200–600K visits**, putting its religion section in hadithbd.com's band. That range is an illustration, not a measurement. What is observed is that newspapers rank for seasonal and how-to queries. Jugantor publishes a daily "আজকের নামাজের সময়সূচি" (today's prayer schedule) article ([Jugantor](https://www.jugantor.com/islam-life)), and jagonews24.com appeared in 6 of 16 Bing-BD Islamic results pages. Dedicated sites, by contrast, hold #1 positions on several evergreen reference terms; hadithbd ranks #1 for সাইয়েদুল ইস্তেগফার (Sayyidul Istighfar) and কোরবানির ফজিলত সম্পর্কে হাদিস (hadith on the virtues of Qurbani) ([Ahrefs hadithbd](https://ahrefstop.com/websites/hadithbd.com)).

## Dead domains and moved sites

Several domains that look like natural Bangla Islamic properties are not active sites:

- **islamijibon.com** redirects to "IslamiJibon.com is for sale | HugeDomains" ([islamijibon.com](https://islamijibon.com)).
- **quranbangla.com** is also for sale ([quranbangla.com](https://quranbangla.com)).
- **al-hadith.com** is a parked "/lander" page ([al-hadith.com](https://al-hadith.com)).
- **hadith.one** timed out twice (25 s and 20 s) during the audit, yet it still appears among Bing-BD results for "hadith bangla" ([hadith.one](https://hadith.one); [Bing hadith bangla](https://www.bing.com/search?q=hadith%20bangla&cc=BD&setlang=bn)).
- **salat.habibur.com** shows a banner, "OUR LATEST SITE IS HERE >> namaz.habibur.com", so its traffic may be migrating ([salat.habibur.com](https://salat.habibur.com/)).
- **assunnahfoundation.org** is a charity/NGO site with no Quran, hadith, dua or prayer-time content ([ASF](https://assunnahfoundation.org/)).

From earlier research (Nov 4 2026), a web-only entrant, **Priyo Muslim**, launched in September 2026 with prayer times for all 64 districts ([goodnews.com.bd](https://goodnews.com.bd/priyo-muslim-islamic-app/)). It was not re-checked in this round and has no traffic figure.

## Conclusion

The ranking shows a thin market. The second-largest Bangla Islamic site has about 600K estimated Bangladesh visits in a country of roughly 75M Muslim internet users (see [report 01](01-bangladesh-islamic-digital-market.md)). No dedicated site holds a dominant position against newspapers, YouTube or quran.com. The leaders are also lopsided: muslimbangla is broad but unsourced, while hadithbd is deep but has no prayer times. Volume estimates for the same site differ by 2–3× across tools, so plans should be built on rank order and keyword positions, not on absolute visits. The single most useful next measurement would be first-party data: from a partner site, or from our own site once launched.

## Confidence & limitations

- **High:** relative order of Tier 1 vs Tier 2; organic search as the dominant channel; keyword themes.
- **Medium:** implied Bangladesh visits for Tier 1 and 2 (single tool, ambiguous label, ±2–3×).
- **Low:** salat.habibur.com (desktop-only geography, no rank); islamijindegi, quranmazid and islamhouse (tiny or inseparable samples); every global-benchmark Bangladesh figure (upper bounds only).
- **Not fetched (no numbers):** islamicfinder.org, hamariweb.com, timeanddate, salahtimes.com, sunnah.com, assunnahfoundation.org traffic, islamicfoundation.gov.bd, quran.gov.bd (re-verify), islamqa.info/bn, bangla.islamonweb.net, al-itisam.com, tawheederdak.com, ruqyahbd.org, muftialamin.com, wafilife.com, namaz.habibur.com, Priyo Muslim, and newspaper domain totals other than prothomalo.
- **Not on free tiers:** full channel split, top pages, subfolder traffic, and Bangladesh share outside the top 5. Semrush, Hypestat, Siterankdata and Statshow were not tried for most sites. See [methodology](10-research-methodology-and-sources.md).

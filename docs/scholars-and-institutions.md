# Scholars and institutions: a shortlist for content review and partnership

- Date: 4 November 2026. Purpose: people and bodies to approach later for **content review**, **endorsement** or **content licences**. See `product-requirements.md` §10.
- **Before contacting anyone, check the current status of every name.** Roles change, and some titles below conflict between sources; each entry says which.

## How to use this list

1. **Most well-known scholars will not review app screens themselves.** The realistic route:
   - approach their **institution** (madrasa, foundation, darul ifta), or
   - ask a qualified student or mufti they recommend.

   Their endorsement, or their institution's name, is what users trust.
2. **Each country needs reviewers for its main madhhabs.** Bangladesh needs Hanafi and Ahle Hadith/Salafi. Indonesia needs Shafi. Kerala (India) needs Shafi.
3. **Use different people for different jobs:**
   - a **mufti** checks fiqh (wudu and salah steps)
   - a **muhaddith** checks hadith grades
   - a **qari with ijazah** checks the recitation course and audio
4. Keep a written record of every review: who reviewed, what, and when.

## Due-diligence checklist (before naming anyone in the app)

- [ ] Current role confirmed from the institution's own site or recent news.
- [ ] Qualifications fit the job: fiqh needs a mufti, hadith a muhaddith, recitation a qari with ijazah.
- [ ] No unresolved legal or reputational issue that would hurt trust in the app.
- [ ] Written agreement covering:
  - what they reviewed
  - whether their name may be shown
  - payment, or a sadaqah jariyah arrangement
- [ ] A balance across madhhabs for the target country, so the app is not seen as one group's app.

# Top Islamic websites by Bangladesh traffic, and Bangladesh digital market context (accessed 2026-11-05)

**Read this first.**

- **All traffic numbers are third-party estimates, not measured analytics.**
  - Similarweb free pages show data for September 2026, all fetched 2026-11-05.
  - Ahrefs figures come from the free ahrefstop.com pages, which show October 2026 data, fetched 2026-11-05.
  - No site owner publishes analytics.
- **What the Similarweb headline "Total Visits" means.** The fetched page text puts this figure beside a "Total Visits Last 3 Months" chart, so it could be either a 3-month total or the latest month.
  - I treat it as the **latest single month (Sept 2026)**. Two reasons:
    - For quran.com, 19.7M as one month fits Ahrefs' separate 6.3M organic-only figure plus quran.com's 46.9% direct share.
    - That is Similarweb's standard free-page layout.
  - If it is actually a 3-month total, divide every Similarweb "implied BD visits" figure below by 3. This does not change the ranking order.
- **How "implied BD visits" is calculated:** global visits × Bangladesh share, both taken from the same tool and month.
- **What Ahrefs measures:** organic search traffic only, so it is not comparable to Similarweb's all-channel totals.

## Q1. Which Islamic websites get meaningful traffic from Bangladesh, and how do they rank?

### Takeaway
- **The leaders are two Bangla-first sites, both around 0.6–0.9M BD visits/month:**
  - muslimbangla.com: about 906K implied BD visits/month, Similarweb BD rank #460.
  - hadithbd.com: about 601K, BD rank #478.
- **The next tier is about 100–360K BD visits/month:**
  - salat.habibur.com (prayer times; desktop-only data)
  - alqurans.com (Bangla Quran)
  - alkawsar.com (fatwa/magazine)
  - ihadis.com (IRD hadith)
- **Global giants have unknown BD volume.** For quran.com and islamqa.info, Bangladesh is below their top-5 countries, so only an upper bound is known.
- **The single biggest search-demand theme is prayer times (নামাজের সময়সূচি).** It drives muslimbangla.com and salat.habibur.com and appears in ihadis.com's top keywords. It is followed by dua (Sayyidul Istighfar, Istikhara), popular surahs (Mulk, Waqiah, Kahf) and seasonal topics (Dhul-Hijjah, Qurbani).

### Inferences
- **Two clear tiers.** Tier 1 is muslimbangla.com and hadithbd.com, each with roughly 0.6–0.9M BD visits/month and a BD rank around #460–480. Everything else Bangla-native is at least 3x smaller.
- **The likely biggest single Islamic site used from BD is quran.com,** but this cannot be confirmed. Its upper bound (<1.14M BD visits) overlaps Tier 1.
- **Search intent is utilitarian:**
  - Prayer times dominate the head keywords: "নামাজের সময়সূচি" at 37K volume and "ফজরের নামাজের সময়" at 31K (Ahrefs BD volumes).
  - Next come dua/zikr pages, single-surah pages with Bangla meaning, Islamic baby names, and seasonal ibadah content (Dhul-Hijjah, Qurbani, Laylatul Qadr, Mawlid).
  - A product that wins "prayer time + city" and "surah X bangla" queries addresses the largest demand pools.
- **Organic search is the #1 channel for nearly every Bangla Islamic site** (48–66% of desktop visits). The exceptions are quraanshareef.org and islamijindegi.com, which are direct-led and app-linked.
  - Social referrals come from Facebook, WhatsApp and YouTube.
  - SEO is the main acquisition lever for a Bangla Islamic web product.
- **The audience splits by madhhab or movement.** Ahl-e-Hadith sites (at-tahreek, al-itisam, tawheederdak) cluster with hadithbd. Deobandi/Qawmi-associated sites (alkawsar, muslimbangla) cluster together. Both clusters still overlap. The madhhab labels are inference, not verified by these sources.
- **Absolute volumes are uncertain** by a factor of about 2–3 because the tools disagree. The rank order is the more reliable output.

## Q2. Where do Bangladeshi news portals' Islam sections sit relative to dedicated Islamic sites?

### Takeaway
- **Bangladeshi news portals are one to two orders of magnitude bigger than any dedicated Islamic site.** prothomalo.com alone has about 20M implied BD visits/month, Similarweb BD rank #10.
- **Their Islam/religion sections' traffic cannot be measured with free tools.** Whether a dharma section beats muslimbangla.com is therefore unknown.
- **What the evidence does show:**
  - Similarweb finds strong audience overlap between at-tahreek.com and news portals: jagonews24 85%, prothomalo 83%, somoynews.tv 80%.
  - None of prothomalo's visible top keywords are religious.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **News portals capture some Islamic search demand,** but only for some queries. They can win ranking for seasonal and news-hook queries (Ramadan timetables, Eid dates, Hajj news, Shab-e-Barat), helped by domain authority (prothomalo is BD #10).
- **Dedicated sites probably own evergreen reference queries:** dua text, hadith, surah meaning and daily prayer times. This is supported by the Ahrefs keyword positions above, where hadithbd ranks #1 for several such terms. That support is an inference from limited keyword samples.
- **Even 1–3% of prothomalo's BD traffic** (a hypothetical range, not measured) would equal 200–600K visits. That would put a large portal's Islam section in the same band as hadithbd.com.

### Gaps
- No section-level traffic exists for Prothom Alo ধর্ম, Kaler Kantho ইসলাম, Jugantor ইসলাম ও জীবন, Dhaka Post ইসলাম or Jago News ধর্ম. Getting it requires Semrush/Ahrefs subfolder reports, which are paid.
- jugantor.com, kalerkantho.com, dhakapost.com and jagonews24.com domain totals were not fetched (budget).

## Q3. Bangladesh digital market context (internet, mobile, Muslim share, language)

### Takeaway
Bangladesh has about 176M people (UN, Oct 2025), of whom about 91% are Muslim (BBS Census 2022). Two measures of internet reach disagree widely:
- **DataReportal/Kepios:** 82.8M unique internet users (47.0%) at end-2025.
- **BTRC:** 135.94M internet *subscriptions* in June 2026, about 89% of them mobile.

The gap is between unique people and SIM-based subscriptions. Connectivity is overwhelmingly mobile by subscription. However, StatCounter's Oct 2026 page-view sample oddly shows desktop at 56% / mobile at 43.6%.

### Inferences
- **The addressable Muslim internet audience is roughly 75M people.** This is about 91% × 82.8M unique users, a back-of-envelope figure assuming equal internet penetration by religion. It is not sourced as such.
- **StatCounter's desktop-majority reading conflicts with BTRC's ~89% mobile subscription share.** StatCounter's reading is likely an artefact of its tracker-site sample or of bot/desktop-heavy traffic. Do **not** use it to claim Bangladeshi web users are desktop-first.
  - Similarweb's free traffic-source percentages are desktop-only, which adds to the uncertainty.
  - Design for mobile-first.
- **Products must handle both Bangla-script and Banglish queries,** because the BD search demand above uses both.

### Gaps
- **Bangla-language share of web search** in Bangladesh: no source found. Google does not publish it, and DataReportal does not report language.
- **StatCounter mobile/desktop history for 2025–2026** was not pulled. Without it, the desktop spike cannot be confirmed as an anomaly versus a trend.
- **BTRC primary tables** (btrc.gov.bd) were not fetched directly. These figures come via news outlets.
- **DataReportal's mobile share of web traffic** (its own StatCounter-sourced chart) was not shown in the fetched text.

**How the data was collected, and what each evidence type means**
- **observed_suggestion**: Google Autocomplete, `https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=bn&gl=bd&q=<seed>` (Bangla seeds), plus `hl=en&gl=bd` for Latin-script seeds. I ran 712 seed queries in two rounds, with 0 failures. A suggestion shows that a query exists. It is **not** search volume.
- **trends_relative**: Google Trends, Bangladesh, `today 12-m`, weekly data, pulled through the public Trends explore/widget API ([trends.google.com/trends/explore?geo=BD&date=today%2012-m](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?geo=BD&date=today%2012-m)). Each comparison set of 5 terms included the anchor **"নামাজের সময়"** (prayer time). Values are written as "**N.NNx anchor**", meaning the term's 52-week mean divided by the anchor's 52-week mean in the same set. These are relative numbers only. Several sets were rate-limited (HTTP 429). The sets for Islamic name vs English names (g4) and Hajj package/Umrah/Hajj registration (g6) never returned. "Trends related N" is the score on Google's 0–100 related-queries list for the named seed.
- **serp_observation**: Google results pages `https://www.google.com/search?hl=bn&gl=bd&q=<query>`, fetched inside the user's own Chrome in Bangladesh (signed in, so results may be personalised). I sampled 34 queries. For each I recorded the visible site names, the People-Also-Ask questions, and a count of `youtube.com/watch` links in the page HTML (a rough measure of how video-heavy the results are). DuckDuckGo (`html.duckduckgo.com/html/?kl=bd-en`) returned results for 2 queries before it blocked me (HTTP 403). Bing returned no parseable results.
- **measured_volume**: **none collected.** Semrush, Ahrefs, Ubersuggest, keywordtool.io and Google Keyword Planner all need a login or render volumes client-side, so I could not get absolute volume or difficulty. The `search_volume`, `keyword_difficulty` and `competition` columns are therefore `n/a` in every row, rather than estimated.

## Q1. What do Bangladeshi users actually type, by sub-topic and script?

### Takeaway
Most queries are typed in **Bangla script**. Of the 582 rows, 498 are Bangla, 34 Banglish, 26 English and 24 mixed. Banglish and English show up mainly for prayer and sehri/iftar *times* (namaz time dhaka, iftar time today mirpur), Hajj/Umrah *packages and visas*, baby *names*, and *captions/DP images*. Bangla queries follow a few fixed shapes: **"<topic> এর নিয়ম"** (how-to), **"<X> কয়টি ও কি কি"** (list; often homework), **"<X> কি জায়েজ / হারাম / হালাল নাকি হারাম"** (ruling), **"<X> করলে কি গুনাহ হবে / না পড়লে কি গুনাহ হবে"** (sin check), **"<year> / <district>"** suffixes on time-sensitive pages, and **"<topic> <scholar name / আল কাউসার>"** (looking for a trusted named authority).

Seasonality is covered by another researcher; these notes give only peak weeks.
- Head terms in this scope with clear demand are prayer times (Bangla and Banglish together reach about 2x anchor), Islamic names (about 0.5x), sehri/iftar time (about 0.33x each, in Ramadan only), and the generic ruling words. ### Gaps
- No absolute monthly volumes, keyword difficulty or CPC are available. Semrush, Ahrefs, Ubersuggest, keywordtool.io and Keyword Planner all need a login or render client-side. Any volume figure would be a fabrication.
- No Trends data for Hajj/Umrah packages or for gendered name terms because of HTTP 429. A retry after a cool-down, or a manual Trends UI check, is needed.
- Trends excludes low-volume phrases (zeros), so long-tail Q&A demand is under-measured.

## Q3. What question patterns recur?

### Takeaway
Seven templates account for most Q&A-style queries. Each maps to a repeatable answer format: **ruling** ("… কি জায়েজ / হারাম / হালাল নাকি হারাম / জায়েজ কিনা / জায়েজ আছে কিনা"), **sin-check** ("… করলে / না পড়লে / না দিলে কি গুনাহ হবে"), **validity** ("… দিলে কি নামাজ হবে / তালাক হয়"), **how-to** ("… এর নিয়ম / পড়ার নিয়ম / করার নিয়ম ও দোয়া / নিয়ম ও নিয়ত"), **list/count** ("… কয়টি ও কি কি / কত রাকাত / কত প্রকার"), **amount-this-year** ("… কত টাকা ২০২৬ / কবে ২০২৭ / কত তারিখে বাংলাদেশ"), and **authority-tagged** ("… আল কাউসার / শায়খ আহমাদুল্লাহ / মিজানুর রহমান আজহারী / আহলে হক মিডিয়া / দলিলসহ / সহীহ হাদিস").

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- A Q&A product can be organised around these seven templates. Each answer page can carry a fixed structure: short ruling, evidence, madhhab differences, and a named reviewer. That structure meets the explicit demand for "দলিলসহ / সহীহ হাদিস / আল কাউসার".
- The authority tags suggest users do not trust anonymous answers and search for a *person* or *publication*. A new entrant needs visible scholar attribution to compete.

### Gaps
- Autocomplete is prefix-based, so the full range of "...কি জায়েজ" subjects is not exhaustively enumerable. The lists above are samples, not complete inventories.

### Inferences
- The weakest SERPs relative to demand are (a) **calculators** (zakat and faraid: foreign, charity-run or thin tools, with no strong Bangla, BD-law-aware tool), (b) **captions, quotes and names** (Pinterest, Medium, SEO blogs, Indian sites; no sourcing of quoted hadith or ayat), (c) **intimacy and women's fiqh** (Facebook posts dominate private questions), (d) **finance rulings** (Facebook, YouTube Forex channels, blogs), and (e) **prophets' stories and children's learning** (video and PDF piracy).
- muslimbangla.com is the strongest generalist incumbent. Newspapers' Islam sections rank on authority but answer in a news format. Ahl-e-Hadith monthlies (at-Tahreek, al-Itisam) and Hanafi sources (Al-Kawsar, Ahle Haq Media) compete on the same query, so users see conflicting rulings with no madhhab labelling. A labelled multi-view answer is a clear opportunity.
- The SERPs heaviest in YouTube (ruqyah 33, music ruling 29, prophets' stories 19, Arabic 19, namaz er niyom 16, trading 15, Eid prayer 14, qurbani 13) show where demand is for video. A text-only site will struggle there unless it embeds or produces video.

### Gaps
- These SERPs come from one signed-in browser on one day (personalised, not rank-tracked). No positions over time and no mobile vs desktop comparison.
- DuckDuckGo blocked automated access after 2 queries, and Bing HTML/RSS returned nothing parseable, so cross-engine comparison is minimal.
- I did not open result pages to grade answer quality (sourcing, madhhab labelling) page by page. The "poor result" judgement rests on the type and identity of the domains ranking.

# Leading Global Islamic Apps and Their Performance in Bangladesh (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-04. Store figures (ratings, installs, prices) are snapshots and change often. Install and MAU numbers from AppGoblin, AppBrain and App Pricing Lab are third-party estimates. "Users" figures in store copy are developer marketing claims. Similarweb Bangladesh charts are dated **November 1, 2026** unless noted.

## 1. Which global apps lead the category? (profiles)

### Takeaway
The global leaders split into four groups. **Muslim Pro** (Bitsmedia, Singapore) is the all-in-one, ad-plus-subscription giant with about 150M Android installs. **WeMuslim** (Metaverse Technology FZ-LLC) is a fast-growing all-in-one launched in 2023. It claims about 100M Android installs and is now **#1 in Bangladesh's Lifestyle chart**. The nonprofit Quran readers are **Quran.com / Quran Foundation** (82M+ yearly active users across web and mobile) and **Al Quran by Greentech** (14.7M+ installs, no ads). The specialists are **Tarteel** (AI hifz, 15M+ claimed users), **Athan by IslamicFinder** (prayer times) and **Pillars** (privacy-first prayer app). In Bangladesh, local Bangla-first apps beat most global apps. Muslim Pro is only #48 in Lifestyle, and Quran.com and Tarteel are not in the top charts at all.

## 2. What features made them popular?

### Takeaway
Popularity rests first on dependable **prayer times, athan and Qibla**. Retention comes from **Quran plus translation and audio**, then **tracking, streaks and gamification**. The newest differentiator is **AI recitation correction** (Tarteel). Widgets and Watch support are marketed but buggy at scale (Muslim Pro reviews). Offline, ad-free Quran readers (Greentech) win on ratings.

### Cited Findings
- Muslim Pro's feature stack includes a prayer and fasting tracker with streaks, gamified "Quests (Stars & Crescents)", a halal restaurant and prayer-space finder, journaling, an "Ummah" community, a Ramadan Companion, Deen Mode, Premium widgets and an Apple Watch app — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/muslim-pro-quran-prayer-times/id388389451)
- Tarteel's AI mistake detection, hifz Test Mode, peeking and hidden-verse modes, goal planning and voice ayah search — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tarteel-ai-quran/id1391009396). AI detection is word-level and tashkeel-level. Letter-level tajweed detection was not part of the original launch — [Tarteel blog](https://tarteel.ai/blog/introducing-mistake-detection/)
- One 2025 guide says Tarteel catches obvious recitation errors but misses subtle ones such as madd length — [Quran in Depth (blog)](https://www.quranindepth.com/blog/top-quran-apps-2025)
- Greentech offers word-by-word translation, tafsir and audio translation, with no ads — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.greentech.quran)
- Pillars differentiates on on-device prayer-time calculation and no ads — [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/ikrd/pillars-app)
- Muslim Pro added video and entertainment through Qalbox (2022) — [The Vibes](https://www.thevibes.com/articles/culture/79020/muslim-pro-app-launches-global-streaming-service-qalbox-in-malaysia)

### Inferences
- Tajweed-level AI feedback, especially for Bangla speakers, is still an open frontier. Tarteel's own docs show word-level detection, and Tarteel does not list Bengali as a UI language.
- Super-app breadth (Muslim Pro) brings bloat and reliability complaints. The higher-rated apps are focused ones.

### Gaps
- No quantitative retention or engagement data by feature (Sensor Tower and data.ai are paywalled).
- I did not confirm smartwatch support for WeMuslim, Greentech or Quran.com.

## 3. How do they monetize and what do premium tiers cost?

### Takeaway
There are three models. **Freemium with ads plus subscription**: Muslim Pro, WeMuslim, Athan. **Premium subscription for AI and power features**: Tarteel, the most expensive at about $99/yr. **Free, donation-funded nonprofit**: Quran.com and Greentech, with no ads and no IAP. Pillars sits between: ad-free with optional premium. Prayer-time apps sell at around $10–35/yr in the US. AI hifz commands about $99–240/yr.

A free trial is offered — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tarteel-ai-quran/id1391009396). ### Gaps
- No Bangladesh-specific (BDT) price points found for Muslim Pro, Tarteel or WeMuslim.
- No verified revenue figures. AppGoblin's estimate is a rough third-party figure.

## 5. Notable controversies and lessons

### Takeaway
The defining controversy is **Muslim Pro's November 2020 location-data scandal**. Motherboard (Vice) reported that the app sent user location data to the broker X-Mode, whose data reached US military contractors. Muslim Pro denied selling to the military but cut ties with all data brokers. French users sued. The lesson: a faith app's trust is its moat, and privacy-first positioning (Pillars, Greentech, Quran.com) is now a competitive weapon.

### Cited Findings
- Motherboard found Muslim Pro among hundreds of apps sending location data to X-Mode, which sold to US military customers. The data included Wi-Fi network name, timestamp and device info. The Muslim Mingle dating app also sent data to X-Mode — [Common Dreams](https://www.commondreams.org/news/2020/11/16/absolutely-sickening-us-military-buys-location-data-harvested-apps-including-one); [TBS News (Bangladesh)](https://www.tbsnews.net/world/us-military-buys-location-data-popular-muslim-apps-report-159190)
- Muslim Pro called the reports "untrue" and said data was anonymized. It terminated relationships with X-Mode and all other data partners, saying it had worked with X-Mode for only about four weeks — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2020/11/18/muslim-pro-app-denies-selling-user-data-to-us-military); [The Rakyat Post](https://www.therakyatpost.com/news/malaysia/2020/11/18/muslim-pro-announces-it-will-stop-sharing-location-data-after-finding-out-u-s-military-connection/)
- French users sued Muslim Pro over the data sharing — [Middle East Eye](https://acquiaprod.middleeasteye.net/node/192571). A US rights group (CAIR) urged Congress to investigate — [Middle East Eye](https://acquiaprod.middleeasteye.net/node/249441)
- The scandal was covered in Bangladeshi media at the time — [TBS News](https://www.tbsnews.net/world/us-military-buys-location-data-popular-muslim-apps-report-159190)
- Muslim Pro's current Android build still carries 10 ad-network SDKs plus SDKs labeled Tencent, Volcengine (ByteDance) and Alipay — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.bitsmedia.android.muslimpro). This is SDK detection only, not evidence of data sale.
- Pillars was explicitly built in response, with on-device location processing — [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/ikrd/pillars-app)

### Inferences
- Lessons for a new app: avoid third-party location and data brokers entirely; compute prayer times on-device; publish a plain-language privacy policy (in Bangla too); minimize ad SDKs, or use only contextual, non-tracking ads.
- "Never sell your data" can be a headline marketing claim in Bangladesh, where the 2020 story was reported locally.

### Gaps
- I did not establish the outcome of the French lawsuit.
- I found no controversies specific to WeMuslim or other BD apps (e.g. ad-fraud or permission abuse) in this pass.

## 6. Category trends 2024–2026

### Takeaway
Three trends stand out. **AI** now runs through the category: recitation correction (Tarteel), AI "Aalim" chat assistants, and AI branding even in BD charts. **Nonprofit, ad-free and privacy-first** apps are gaining (Quran.com at 82M+ yearly users). And **hifz/Quran learning and content (video, kids)** are expanding. Venture funding in the niche remains tiny.

### Cited Findings
- Quran.com growth: 63M+ web active users (Mar 2024 to Mar 2025) rose to 82M+ web and mobile (Feb 2025 to Feb 2026). Native mobile reached 5M+ monthly users — [Quran Foundation 2025 YIR](https://quran.foundation/year-in-review/2025); [Quran Foundation](https://quran.foundation/)
- Tarteel promotes "Tarteel Alim" and AI-based Premium. Its user claims rose to "15+ million" — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tarteel-ai-quran/id1391009396); [Tarteel Support](https://support.tarteel.ai/en/articles/12414387-what-s-tarteel-premium)
- AI-branded Islamic apps rank in BD (e.g. "Quran, Aalim AI, Prayer, Qibla" at #35 Lifestyle) — [Similarweb BD Lifestyle](https://www.similarweb.com/top-apps/google/bangladesh/lifestyle/)
- 2025 guides describe AI chatbots for faith Q&A, AI recitation feedback and personalized reminders. They also note a shift toward all-in-one apps and toward donation-supported, ad-free apps such as Quran.com and Mawaqit — [Quran in Depth](https://www.quranindepth.com/blog/best-islamic-apps-2025)
- Funding is thin. Tracxn counts only 4 funded Islamic prayer-app startups with about $500K raised in total (Guider is top-funded) — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/trending-business-models/startups-in-islamic-prayer-apps/__3X7xz4vxu0rHB963MtPMzMsisf3A5fWBQHNKuhlTHkc).

# Islamic Web Platforms and Open Data/APIs (Bangladesh-first, global context), as of Nov 2026

Note on traffic data: every traffic figure below is a third-party **estimate** (Similarweb or Ahrefs free pages), not measured analytics. Similarweb free pages sometimes show numbers without labels; where that happened it is flagged. Data months: Similarweb Sept 2026 (fetched 2026-11-04) unless noted; Ahrefs page undated (captured 2026).

## 1. Which Bangla Islamic websites are biggest?

### Takeaway
The Bangla Islamic web is a cluster of mid-sized sites, with no global-scale player. hadithbd.com and muslimbangla.com lead (Similarweb Bangladesh country rank ~#460-480; Ahrefs estimates ~255K organic visits/month each). Behind them come alqurans.com, ihadis.com (IRD Foundation), alkawsar.com, at-tahreek.com, and the government quran.gov.bd. Global sites (quran.com, sunnah.com, islamqa.info) show up as close "competitors" to the Bangla sites, so Bangladeshi users also use them, but Bangladesh isn't in quran.com's top-5 countries.

### Inferences
- **No dominant Bangla web player.** Each top Bangla site gets roughly a quarter-million organic visits a month, against 6M+ for quran.com. A well-built Bangla web/PWA could plausibly rank among the top Bangla Islamic sites within a modest SEO effort.
- **The leading Bangla sites are content-first** (hadith text, fatwa Q&A, articles), not tool-first. Prayer times and Quran reading on the web in Bangladesh likely go to global players or to apps.
- **muslimbangla.com lost organic traffic** (−73.7K per Ahrefs). That may reflect Google AI Overviews or algorithm changes affecting Q&A sites. This is speculative; the cause isn't stated.
- **at-tahreek.com and ahlehadith.** at-tahreek.com is the monthly Ahle Hadith magazine, and its high affinity score with hadithbd.com suggests a shared Salafi/Ahle-Hadith audience. Taken with muslimbangla.com (Deobandi/Hanafi-leaning), this suggests a madhhab split in content choices that a new app must handle. The magazine identity and madhhab positioning are not verified by sources in these notes.

### Gaps
- **No Bangladesh share for global sites.** I couldn't get Bangladesh traffic share for quran.com, sunnah.com, islamqa.info or islamicfinder.org: Bangladesh isn't in the free top-5 lists, and full country lists need a Similarweb account.
- **No reliable monthly visit totals for the Bangla sites.** Similarweb showed unlabeled numbers only.
- **Not covered due to the tool-call budget:**
  - Ownership and monetization of hadithbd.com (believed to be run by Bangladesh Islamic Center / Sunnah Foundation-linked volunteers — unverified), muslimbangla.com, alqurans.com and alkawsar.com (Markazud Dawah Al-Islamia monthly — unverified).
  - IRD's other web apps (Dua & Ruqyah, Quran Mazid, Tafsir) and their URLs.
  - islamqa.info's Bangla section (islamqa.info/bn), its size and traffic.
  - Bangla waz/lecture sites (YouTube is likely dominant).
  - IslamHouse Bangla traffic.
- **UX not audited firsthand** for the Bangla sites: no data on PWA support, offline use or accessibility.

## 2. Which global web apps dominate?

### Takeaway
quran.com is by far the largest Islamic web app: Similarweb global rank ~#1,280 and 19.7M visits over 3 months to Sept 2026. Its traffic comes mostly from the US/UK/Pakistan/Malaysia/Indonesia. The next tier is islamqa.info (#8,228), islamicfinder.org (#18,814, about 4.4M visits over 3 months, North America-heavy) and sunnah.com (#19,551, Pakistan top country). Prayer-time sites (muslimpro.com, timesprayer, salahtimes) cluster with IslamicFinder.

### Cited Findings
- **quran.com** (Quran Foundation), Sept 2026:

  - "Total visits" 19.7M, shown in a last-3-months chart.
  - Top countries: US 23.77%, UK 8.36%, Pakistan 7.57%, Malaysia 6.23%, Indonesia 5.8%, others 48.27%.
  - Bangladesh isn't in the top 5.

  Source: [Similarweb quran.com](https://www.similarweb.com/website/quran.com/)
- **quran.com, Ahrefs:** about 6.3M organic visits/month (+831.5K), DR 82. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefstop.com/websites/muslimbangla.com/competitors)
- **islamicfinder.org**, Sept 2026:
  - Global rank #18,814; about 4.4M visits over the last 3 months.

  - Top competitors: muslimpro.com, timesprayer.com, salahtimes.com, prayertim.es, salattimes.com.

  Source: [Similarweb islamicfinder.org](https://www.similarweb.com/website/islamicfinder.org/)

  - **Hadith:** sunnah.com.
- Bangladeshi users likely reach quran.com and sunnah.com for Arabic/English. But these sites' Bangla coverage is thinner than the local sites', which is the opening for a Bangla-first product.

## 3. What open data/APIs can a new app legally use? (licenses, attribution)

### Takeaway
Licensing is the most decision-relevant finding. The **Quran Foundation (quran.com) API is free and allows monetized apps, but bans caching longer than 1 week and bans prepackaged/bundled databases**. Offline is allowed only via its "Content Sync" mechanism with re-sync every 7 days. That restricts a Bangladesh-first offline mobile app.

The other main sources:
- **Tanzil Quran text** is CC BY 3.0 with no modification allowed, and is safe to bundle with attribution.
- **Tanzil's Bangla translations** (Muhiuddin Khan `bn.bengali`, Zohurul Hoque `bn.hoque`) are **non-commercial only**.
- **QuranEnc** (Abu Bakr Zakaria Bangla) allows republishing under conditions: no changes, attribution, version number, keep it updated, no inappropriate ads.
- **fawazahmed0 hadith-api** is Unlicense, with 8 Bangla collections. Its underlying translation sources may carry their own rights.
- **Adhan** (prayer-time calculation) is MIT.
- **sunnah.com API** requires requesting a key via GitHub. No offline dump is published.

### Cited Findings
**Quran Foundation / Quran.com API (Developer Terms):**
- **License:** non-exclusive, revocable, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, "solely to develop and operate Applications that provide beneficial Quranic experiences to end users." — [QF Developer Terms](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/legal/developer-terms/) (mirror: [api-docs.quran.com](https://api-docs.quran.com/legal/developer-terms/))
- **Monetization:** without a commercial license you may charge for an app, run subscriptions or in-app purchases, show ads, take donations or run freemium, provided:
  - QF content appears only as part of the end-user experience,
  - it isn't sold, sublicensed or redistributed, and
  - you comply with the Terms.

  Source: [QF Developer Terms](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/legal/developer-terms/)
- **Caching:**
  - QF content may not be cached or stored for more than 1 week, except via Content Sync or for fonts/Mushaf images.
  - Content Sync requires re-syncing at least every 7 days when connectivity allows. If offline for more than 7 days, the app may keep using its previous synced copy.
  - Content Sync **does not authorize a prepackaged database or build-time bundle**.

  Source: [QF Developer Terms](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/legal/developer-terms/)
- **Redistribution:** offering QF content or raw data "as data" (your own API, dataset, feed or download) requires a signed commercial license. Serving it from your own backend inside your app is not, by itself, redistribution. Derived materials, including embeddings that allow reconstruction, stay restricted. Scraping outside API responses is prohibited. — [QF Developer Terms](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/legal/developer-terms/)
- **Fonts/Mushaf images:** may be cached or bundled with an active Developer Console account and credit to Quran Foundation. Social videos must credit QF. — [QF Developer Terms](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/legal/developer-terms/)
- **Integrity and enforcement:**
  - Quran text must not be modified, and snippets must not misrepresent meaning.
  - Apps must publish a privacy policy and terms of use.
  - QF can revoke credentials immediately.
  - Liability is capped at the greater of USD 100 or fees paid in the prior 6 months.

  Source: [QF Developer Terms](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/legal/developer-terms/)
- **Translation rights:** the terms only claim QF's rights in content "it owns." Rights in third-party translations aren't addressed, so each translation's source must be checked. The FAQ advises against auto-translating translations. — [QF Developer Terms](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/legal/developer-terms/), [QF FAQ](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/docs/tutorials/faq/)

**Tanzil:**
- **Quran text:** Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. It can be used "in any website or application, provided that its source (Tanzil Project) is clearly indicated." You must link to tanzil.net and keep the copyright notice. "CHANGING IT IS NOT ALLOWED." Commercial use isn't explicitly addressed, but CC BY 3.0 itself permits it. — [Tanzil text license](https://tanzil.net/docs/text_license)
- **Bangla translations on Tanzil:**
  - Muhiuddin Khan (মুহিউদ্দীন খান), file `bn.bengali`.
  - Zohurul Hoque (জহুরুল হক), file `bn.hoque`.
  - Terms: "for non-commercial use only"; other uses need the translator's or publisher's permission. Using more than 3 translations requires a link back to Tanzil.

  Source: [Tanzil translations](https://tanzil.net/trans/)

**QuranEnc (King Fahd Complex / Rowad translation center):**
- Hosts the Abu Bakr Zakaria Bangla translation (key `bengali_zakaria`). — [QuranEnc bengali_zakaria](https://quranenc.com/tl/browse/bengali_zakaria/18/109)
- **Reuse terms:** download and republishing are allowed if you:
  - don't modify, add to or delete from the content,
  - attribute QuranEnc and the publisher,
  - cite the version number and keep transcript info,
  - notify the source of notes,
  - update to the latest version, and
  - show no inappropriate ads.

  Source: [QuranEnc (terms block)](https://quranenc.com/fa/browse/english_rwwad/34/19)
- **API:** GET endpoints for a whole sura or a single aya by translation key. Responses include sura, aya, translation and footnotes. — [QuranEnc](https://quranenc.com/gu/browse/english_yacob/10/1)

**alquran.cloud (Islamic Network):**
- An "open" API, rendered from an OpenAPI spec at api.alquran.cloud, with an audio and image CDN, client libraries and Tajweed support. Run by Islamic Network, with contributors since 2014. Terms are on a linked page I didn't read. — [alquran.cloud API](https://alquran.cloud/api)

**sunnah.com:**
- The API gives access to "a portion of our data." Docs are on Stoplight; you request a key by opening a GitHub issue. An offline dump is "possible but not available yet." — [sunnah.com developers (via search snippet)](https://sunnah.com/developers); key request examples: [GitHub issue #3102](https://github.com/sunnah-com/api/issues/3102), [#3086](https://github.com/sunnah-com/api/issues/3086)
- No explicit data license was found. Third-party repos redistributing sunnah.com data (e.g. CheeseWithSauce/HadithsJSONFormat, MIT) don't settle the upstream rights. — [ecosyste.ms entry](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2FCheeseWithSauce%2FHadithsJSONFormat)

**fawazahmed0/hadith-api:**
- Licensed under the Unlicense (public domain). Free CDN at `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@{version}/{endpoint}.json`. Supports multiple languages and multiple grades. Sources are listed in References.md. — [GitHub hadith-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api)
- **Bangla editions (8):** Abu Dawud, Bukhari, Ibn Majah, Muwatta Malik, Muslim, Nasai, Nawawi 40 and Tirmidhi. There are 10 books in total; Dehlawi 40 and Qudsi 40 have no Bangla edition. — [editions.json](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions.json)

**Prayer times:**
- batoulapps Adhan (Swift, Kotlin/Java, JS) is MIT-licensed. — [ecosyste.ms adhan-js](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2Fbatoulapps%2Fadhan-js), [Maven Central adhan2](https://central.sonatype.com/artifact/com.batoulapps.adhan/adhan2), [npm via depscope](https://depscope.dev/pkg/npm/adhan)
- Aladhan API (same Islamic Network as alquran.cloud): no terms-of-use page was found. A community thread asks about commercial use in an ad-supported app, and no answer was visible. — [Islamic Network community #107](https://community.islamic.network/d/107-can-i-use-aladhan-api-for-commercial-use-in-my-app)

### Inferences
- **Recommended legal stack for an offline-first, Bangla-first web + mobile app:**
  - Bundle the Tanzil Uthmani/Simple Arabic text (CC BY 3.0, attribute and link).
  - Use the QuranEnc Abu Bakr Zakaria Bangla translation (republishing allowed with conditions).
  - Use the fawazahmed0 Bangla hadith (Unlicense, but audit References.md for upstream Bangla translation publishers, e.g. Islamic Foundation or Tawheed Publications).
  - Compute prayer times on-device with Adhan (MIT). On-device calculation avoids any API dependency and works offline.
- **Quran Foundation API fits online-first features** (tafsir, audio streaming, word-by-word, user sync) but can't be the backbone of a bundled offline Quran. Avoid it for the core bundle, or implement Content Sync with 7-day refresh.
- **The most popular Bangla translations (Muhiuddin Khan; Taisirul Quran; Mujibur Rahman) are not cleared for commercial use** on open sources. Tanzil marks Muhiuddin Khan non-commercial, and its print rights are believed held by Islamic Foundation/publishers (unverified). An app with ads or subscriptions needs written permission from the publishers, or must stick to QuranEnc (Zakaria) or a self-commissioned translation.
- **Hadith Bangla text and authorship.** Much Bangla hadith text on the web likely comes from Islamic Foundation Bangladesh and Tawheed Publications editions. Those publishers' copyright status is the key legal risk for hadith content.

### Gaps
- **Not fetched:**
  - Explicit license or permission terms for Taisirul Quran (Tawheed Publications) and Mujibur Rahman (King Fahd Complex print; QuranEnc may host a version).
  - Islamic Foundation Bangladesh's copyright policy on its Quran and hadith translations.
- **Not verified:**
  - Aladhan and alquran.cloud terms text and rate limits.
  - sunnah.com rate limits and data license.
  - fawazahmed0 References.md, which names the upstream source of the Bangla hadith.
  - Quran Foundation's published rate limits and whether its API includes Bangla translations (e.g. Muhiuddin Khan, Taisirul, Rawai al-Bayan) with stated rights.
- **Not covered:** Quran audio licensing (everyayah.com, quranicaudio.com) and HadeethEnc Bangla terms.

## 4. UX/feature quality of these sites vs. apps (search, PWA/offline, accessibility)

### Takeaway
Little hard UX data was collected. What exists suggests that top Bangla sites have mediocre engagement (hadithbd.com: 71.8% bounce rate, about 2.9 pages and under 2 minutes per visit). quran.com is the UX benchmark: an open-source Next.js frontend with an API ecosystem. Offline on the web is contractually limited when using QF content.

### Cited Findings
- **hadithbd.com engagement:** bounce rate 71.81%, 2.92 pages/visit, 1:56 average visit (Similarweb, April 2026). — [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/hadithbd.com/competitors/)
- **ihadis.com features:**
  - Multi-language search including Bangla, and browsing by subject, chapter and grade.
  - Ad-free.
  - The app adds daily hadith notifications (third-party listing).

  Source: [ihadis.com](https://ihadis.com/en), [appwisp](https://appwisp.com/app/android/com.ihadis.ihadis)
- **QF offline rule:** apps using Quran Foundation content can keep offline copies only via Content Sync with 7-day refresh. Web PWAs caching QF content longer than a week would breach the terms. — [QF Developer Terms](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/legal/developer-terms/)
- **QF guidance:** turn off browser or automatic translation of Quran pages, since the API already provides peer-reviewed translations. — [QF FAQ](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/docs/tutorials/faq/)

### Inferences
- **The UX gap in Bangla web is a real opportunity.** Fast Bangla search (including Banglish/phonetic queries), a clean reader, and PWA offline using self-hosted, properly licensed data would stand out against legacy-style Bangla sites. This is based on the high bounce rate and on the local sites being content portals.
- **One codebase is feasible.** A single content backend (Tanzil text + QuranEnc Bangla + Unlicense hadith) can serve web and mobile identically, and avoids QF's caching limits.

### Gaps
- **No firsthand UX audit:**
  - PWA manifest and service-worker checks, Lighthouse/accessibility scores, and Bangla font rendering quality across sites.
  - Bangla search quality (diacritic- and spelling-tolerant) on hadithbd.com, muslimbangla.com and ihadis.com.
- **quran.com specifics not verified here:** the open-source status of quran.com-frontend-next (believed MIT on GitHub at github.com/quran/quran.com-frontend-next) and its PWA/offline capability.

# Bangladesh-origin / Bangla-language Islamic Apps: Competitive Catalog (as of Nov 2026)

**How the data was gathered (read this first).** The install, rating, review and last-update figures were scraped directly from Google Play listing pages on 2026-11-04 (`hl=en&gl=BD`). Google Play shows a rounded bucket (e.g. "5,000,000+"). The page's embedded data also carries an unrounded install number, listed below as "~exact". Treat it as Google's own approximate counter, not an audited figure. "Ads" means the listing shows "Contains ads"; "IAP" means it shows "In-app purchases". Each app's source is its Play URL: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=<package>`. AppBrain developer pages returned HTTP 403, and Sensor Tower and data.ai charts were not available, so no country-level ranking data from those sources is included.

## Q1. Which Bangladeshi Islamic apps have the most installs/users?

### Takeaway
On Google Play, the biggest Bangla-focused Islamic apps are Greentech's **Al Quran (Tafsir & by Word)** (~14.7M, but used worldwide), kutblog's **Bangla Quran** (~8.7M), TopOfStack's **Muslim Bangla** (~6.0M) and Kahf's **Muslims Day**, formerly App of Ramadan (~5.7M). Behind them sit about 15 apps in the 1M–4M range: IRD's Al Hadith, Greentech's Bangla Hisnul Muslim, iT Solution's Namaj Shikkha, Technobd Al-Quran and others. All of them are far below global players Muslim Pro (~149M) and Wemu (~130M), both of which ship Bangla localization.

### Inferences
- On Bangladesh-specific breadth plus ratings, the clear leader is **Muslim Bangla**: a 4.9 rating across 147K reviews and the widest feature set. Greentech's Quran app has more installs, but a large share of them come from outside Bangladesh, because it supports 66 languages.
- The market is split into two groups. Large "all-in-one" lifestyle apps (Muslim Bangla, Muslims Day, Namaj Shikkha, Deen variants) compete on daily-use features like prayer times. Single-purpose "authentic text" apps from nonprofits (IRD, GTAF, HadithBD) compete on authenticity and having no ads.
- There is a long tail of hundreds of small "Bangla Quran / Namaz Shikkha" clones. They are mostly ad-supported and under 100K installs, which signals low barriers to entry but also a lot of noise.
- Taken together, the top ~20 BD-targeted apps total roughly 50–60M Play installs (my own sum of the table above). That counts devices, not unique users: users install several apps and reinstall them.

### Gaps
- No Sensor Tower, data.ai or AppBrain country-chart data (AppBrain returned 403), so there is no Bangladesh-only share, DAU/MAU or chart rank.
- iOS ratings and download counts were not collected for most apps. iOS has a small share of the Bangladesh market (my inference, unverified here).
- Play installs are worldwide, not Bangladesh-only.

## Q2. What features does each app offer? (Per-app profiles)

### Takeaway
The standard feature set is almost the same everywhere: Quran with Bangla translation and pronunciation, prayer times with azan, qibla, tasbih, dua, a Hijri calendar, Sehri/Iftar times and a zakat calculator. The apps differ on **depth of authentic text** (IRD, GTAF, HadithBD), **human services** (Muslim Bangla's scholar Q&A, live mufti calls, matrimony and quizzes with prizes), **halal media** (Kahf's Mahfil and Kahf Kids) and **learning paths** (Makhraj/Nurani, Namaz Shikkha, Quraner Bhasha).

Several apps carry clear sectarian or organizational markers: Islami Jindegi (Tabligh/Qawmi), Bangladesh Jamaat-e-Islami's Tafheemul Quran, Quantum Foundation's "Mormobani", and Ruqyah Support BD.

## Q4. How are they monetized and are they ad-heavy?

### Takeaway
There are three models. (1) **Ad-free nonprofit, funded by donations**: IRD, GTAF, HadithBD, Technobd, Islami Jindegi. (2) **Ads plus paid ad removal or premium**: Muslim Bangla, kutblog, Namaj Shikkha, Muslims Day, most small clones. (3) **Subscriptions**, some billed in Taka per day: Deen Islamic, Muslim Pro BD and the telco VAS model. Users are used to the Taka-per-day micro-subscription model, but complaints about auto-renewal show the trust risk it carries.

### Cited Findings
- Muslim Bangla: ads plus IAP. On iOS the IAPs are "Full Screen Ads Removal" at $4.99 and "Banner Ads Removal" at $4.99 (R99.99 each on the South Africa store). No premium subscription was confirmed. The website mentions only donations — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/muslim-bangla-quran-salat-dua/id878084956); [App Store ZA](https://apps.apple.com/za/app/muslim-bangla-quran-salat-dua/id878084956); [muslimbangla.com](https://muslimbangla.com/). Additional revenue lines appear to be zakat/sadaqa services, a matrimony site (muslimakd.com) and e-commerce (muslimponno.com) — [muslimbangla.com](https://muslimbangla.com/)
- Deen Islamic (Maverick): iOS IAP Monthly $1.99, Yearly $9.99, Lifetime $49.99. Premium includes offline Quran download and an ad-free experience. A Play reviewer reports being charged "3 tk daily" and being unable to cancel auto-renewal (a single anecdote). The privacy policy confirms auto-renewal and a payment-gateway provider — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/deen-quran-hadith-prayer-time/id6504724978); [Privacy policy](https://deenislamic.com/privacy_policy.html); [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.deenislamic&hl=en_US)
- Grameenphone runs its own carrier-billed Islamic VAS: dial 22200, BDT 2/day auto-renew plus taxes — [Grameenphone](https://www.grameenphone.com/personal/services/vas/islamic-service)
- Muslim Pro's Bangladesh premium starts at ৳5/day — [Muslim Pro BD](https://www.muslimpro.com/bangladesh-premium-page/)

### Inferences
- Bangla reviewers in this category are sensitive to ads ("haram ads", unwanted ads during worship). The ad-free apps (GTAF, IRD) hold 4.7–4.8 ratings at million-plus scale. Ad-supported Muslim Bangla still scores 4.9, so features and community can make up for ads.
- A credible founder strategy: an ad-free core with optional sadaqah/donation or a premium tier for convenience features (sync, offline audio, courses). Daily Taka micro-billing gets attention, but auto-renew complaints are a reputational risk.

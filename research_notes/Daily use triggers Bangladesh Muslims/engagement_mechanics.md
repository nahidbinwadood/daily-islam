# Engagement Mechanics: What Drives Daily and Multiple-Times-a-Day Use in Religious and High-Frequency Apps (Bangladesh Focus, 2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-04. Every claim has an inline source. Labels used throughout: **[Evidence]** means measured or published data (company-reported figures are flagged as such). **[Opinion]** means commentary, vendor marketing or a blog view. **[OLD]** means data from before 2024.

## 1. What engagement data have faith apps published?

### Takeaway
No faith app publishes "opens per user per day". The published numbers are headline DAU, installs, record single days, and plan or challenge sign-ups, almost all company-reported. YouVersion's pattern is the most useful benchmark: a daily ritual (Verse of the Day plus plans) and big seasonal peaks (New Year, Easter, December). Muslim and Quran apps report big user counts but no feature-level engagement. The one usable proxy for which Islamic app features get used is a 2026 survey: Quran apps first, prayer-time apps second, dua and dhikr apps third.

### Cited Findings
**YouVersion (Bible App)**
- [Evidence, company PR] The YouVersion family of apps passed 1 billion installs. In 2025, Bible engagement rose in every global region, including +27% in Sub-Saharan Africa and +33% in the Middle East, Central Asia and North Africa. — [YouVersion 2025 Verse of the Year release, Dec 4 2025, via Webull/PR Newswire](https://www.webull.com/news/13969012967908352); [Premier Christian News](https://premierchristian.news/en/news/article/youversion-names-its-2025-verse-of-the-year-as-global-bible-use-soars)
- [Evidence, company PR] More than 19 million people opened the app on the first Sunday of December 2025, an all-time single-day record. Easter Sunday 2025 reached 18.95 million people, +12% year on year. — [Worthy News](https://www.worthynews.com/104604-youversion-ceo-record-bible-engagement-signals-global-spiritual-awakening); [Christian Post](https://assets.christianpost.com/news/youversion-reveals-top-bible-verse-of-2025.html)
- [Evidence, company PR] On New Year's Day 2025, more than 3 million people subscribed to one-year Bible Plans, +18% over 2024. Plans act as a daily commitment device. — [ChurchLeaders](https://churchleaders.com/news/503794-youversion-bible-app-breaks-download-record.html); [Webull/PR Newswire](https://www.webull.com/news/13969012967908352)
- [Evidence, secondary press, less certain] About 14 million people engage with the Bible in the app every day, and installs average about 11.2 million new devices a month. I could not confirm this in the Dec 2025 release. — [K-LOVE](https://www.klove.com/news/faith/youversion-bible-app-gets-credit-for-helping-people-beat-back-anxiety-48059)
- [Evidence] The 2025 Verse of the Year was Isaiah 41:10 ("fear not, for I am with you"). It was the fourth time in six years that a comfort-in-trials verse topped the list, and the 2024 verse was framed around anxiety. Users come back for emotional reassurance, not doctrine. — [ChurchLeaders 2025](https://churchleaders.com/news/2209898-youversion-2025-verse-of-the-year-bobby-gruenewald.html); [ChurchLeaders 2024](https://churchleaders.com/?p=501782)

**Hallow (Catholic)**
- [Evidence, company] By June 2025: about 24 million installs in more than 150 countries and more than 1 billion prayers completed. — [Hallow blog](https://hallow.com/blog/hallow-celebrates-1-billion-prayers-prayed/)
- [Evidence, third-party estimate] Hallow averaged about 280K downloads a month in 2025 and got 263K downloads on Ash Wednesday 2026 alone. Post-Lent revenue was flat: $10M in March 2024 and $9.7M in April 2025. Seasonal challenges (Pray40 for Lent, Pray25 for Advent) produce predictable spikes, but the spikes do not compound into lasting growth. — [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/hallow-lent-surge-prayer-app-revenue)
- [Evidence, press] More than 1.4 million people joined Hallow's 40-day Lent challenge, as of March 6. The year is not shown in the excerpt; probably 2025. — [ChurchLeaders](https://churchleaders.com/news/507269-million-join-hallow-prayer-challenge-lent.html)
- [Caution] Figures such as "1.8M MAU" and "55% DAU increase" appear only on business-template sites and are unverified. — [businessmodelcanvastemplate.com](https://businessmodelcanvastemplate.com/products/hallow-marketing-mix)

**Muslim Pro**
- [Evidence, company-reported, Oct 2024] 25 million monthly users, 8 million daily active users and 170 million+ downloads. That implies DAU/MAU of about 32% if both figures are accurate. — [New Straits Times, Oct 2024](https://nst.com.my/amp/business/corporate/2024/10/1118337/muslim-pro-owner-seeks-more-ties-regional-global-faith-based)
- [Conflict] AppGoblin's third-party estimate puts MAU at about 3.1M, far below the company's claim. — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.bitsmedia.android.muslimpro)
- [Evidence, product] Muslim Pro markets prayer and fasting streaks and "Gamified Quests" with small rewards. — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/muslim-pro-quran-athan/id388389451)

**Quran.com / Quran Foundation**
- [Evidence, organisation-reported, Mar 2024 to Mar 2025] 63M+ active users and 8.6M+ hours on the Quran.com web app; 5M+ monthly users on the native mobile app since January 2025. Also 130,000+ goals created, 44,000+ learning plans enrolled and 106,000+ notes. Goals and plans reach a tiny share of users (about 0.2% of active users). Notifications and widgets were listed only as future roadmap items. There is no country split and no retention figure. — [Quran Foundation Year in Review 2025](https://quran.foundation/year-in-review/2025)

**Tarteel**
- [Evidence, OLD, Ramadan 1443 AH / 2022] 121,000+ hours of engagement from logged-in users and 25,000+ hours of recitation aloud through the AI feature. — [Tarteel Ramadan Wrapped](https://tarteel.ai/blog/ramadan-wrapped-1443/)

**Pillars**
- [Product description only] Prayer times, Qibla, prayer notifications and a prayer tracker. No usage data published. — [Salaam Gateway](https://salaamgateway.com/story/five-apps-to-help-muslims-stay-focused-in-ramadan)

**Cross-app feature-use proxy**
- [Evidence, self-reported, biased sample, 2026] GTAF "State of the Ummah 1447" surveyed 1,530 Muslims in 87 countries. Bangladesh was n=209 (13.7%), the second-largest country group. Share naming each resource among their most-used Islamic digital resources: Quran apps 95.7%, prayer-time/Adhan apps 59.8%, Dua & Dhikr apps 53.9%, social media for Islamic content 47.2%, lectures/podcasts/video 39.4%, Hadith apps 33%, Q&A/fatwa sites 25.7%. Respondents were recruited mainly through Greentech's own app users and Muslim networks, and the report itself says practising Muslims may be over-represented. — [GTAF State of the Ummah 1447](https://gtaf.org/state-of-the-ummah-1447/)
- [Evidence, same caveat] Bangladeshi respondents reported a 70.8% "daily five-prayer rate". This conflicts with the founder's premise that most Bangladeshi Muslims do not pray five times a day, and it is very likely selection bias: the respondents are users of a Quran app. — [GTAF](https://gtaf.org/state-of-the-ummah-1447/)

### Inferences
- The faith apps that report high daily engagement (YouVersion, Hallow) built it on **one short daily content unit** (Verse of the Day, daily prayer or meditation) plus **time-boxed collective challenges** (one-year plans, 40-day Lent). None reports that users open the app 5+ times a day. Five or more opens a day has no published precedent in faith apps.
- If Muslim Pro's 8M DAU / 25M MAU is accurate, about 32% DAU/MAU is the best available ceiling benchmark for a prayer-time-led Muslim app. That is a once-a-day-ish product, not a 5-times-a-day one.
- Seasonal peaks (Ramadan for Muslim apps, Lent and Easter for Christian ones) are reliable, but Hallow's flat post-Lent revenue suggests the peaks do not carry over without a daily habit hook.

### Gaps
- No faith app publishes opens per day, sessions per user, D1/D7/D30 retention, or feature-level usage (Verse of the Day vs plans vs community).
- No official 2025-2026 Muslim Pro, Pillars or Tarteel engagement data was found. Sensor Tower and data.ai engagement panels are paywalled.
- No Bangladesh-specific engagement data was found for any Islamic app.

## 2. Which mechanics have evidence, and which backfire?

### Takeaway
The strongest published evidence is Duolingo's. Its streak (+1.7% D7 retention from the streak animation; users who reach a 7-day streak are 3.6x more likely to complete their course), its grace mechanic (two Streak Freezes: +0.38% daily active learners without more skipped days), its Leagues (+17% learning time) and a notification bandit (+0.5% DAU, +2% new-user retention) all produced measurable gains. Every one of them sits on a product with inherent daily value. Duolingo also caps notification volume as a rule, because more notifications hurt. Controlled research shows more frequent non-personalised pushes raise uninstalls, and Android opt-in has fallen to roughly 50-67% since the Android 13 permission change. Widgets show about +25% retention for widget adopters (correlational). No public data exists on Live Activities.

### Cited Findings
**Streaks and grace days (Duolingo)**
- [Evidence, OLD 2022] Duolingo: users who reach a 7-day streak are 3.6x more likely to complete their course. The streak-extension animation raised the chance a new learner was still active after 7 days by +1.7%. Letting users equip up to two Streak Freezes raised daily active learners by +0.38% and "did not encourage learners to take more days off". More than 6M learners had 7+ day streaks. — [Duolingo blog, Jan 31 2022](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit)
- [Evidence, 2023] Over four years the share of DAU with a 7+ day streak rose almost 3x, to more than half of DAU. DAU grew 4.5x, CURR (current-user retention) rose 21%, and daily churn among the best users fell more than 40%. The streak-saver notification was the "first big streak win". — [Jorge Mazal, Lenny's Newsletter, Feb 28 2023](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth)
- [Evidence, 2023] Leagues (leaderboards) raised overall learning time by 17% and tripled highly engaged learners. — [Mazal, Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth)
- [Evidence, backfire, 2023] A Gardenscapes-style moves-counter gamification was "completely neutral" for retention and DAU. — [Mazal, Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth)
- [Evidence, backfire] Tying streaks to XP confused users, and simplifying the rule to "one lesson a day" worked better. Making the streak even easier to extend (one exercise) did **not** raise DAU, and the feature mostly captured the least-engaged users. Duolingo's own lesson: "streaks can't compensate for a weak product." Retention is most fragile in the first 7 days. Going from a 1-day to a 2-day streak is the biggest jump. More than 600 streak experiments have been run. — [Lenny's Podcast summary, Jackson Shuttleworth](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/behind-the-product-duolingo-streaks); [Recall summary](https://www.recall.it/summary/lennys-podcast/behind-the-product-duolingo-streaks-or-jackson-shuttleworth-group-pm-retention-team)
- [Evidence] More than 30M DAU, over 70% of DAU active continuously for a week, and about 5M users on year-plus streaks (WSJ via Gigazine, 2024). The "we'll stop sending reminders" message sent after a week of inactivity is reportedly one of the most successful notifications; no numbers given. — [Gigazine, Jun 2024](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20240618-duolingo-app-streaks)
- [Unverified] "Streak Freeze reduced churn 21% for at-risk users" and "streak wager +14% D14 retention" circulate on secondary sites without a primary source. — [Lazyweb](https://lazyweb.com/research/duolingo-retention-experiments); [jtbdux](https://jtbdux-protocol.pages.dev/adoption-and-behavior/behavior-design-patterns/how-duolingo-engineered-50-million-daily-users-and-the-backlash-that-came-with-it)

**Push notifications: timing, frequency, fatigue**
- [Evidence, OLD 2020, peer-reviewed] Duolingo's "sleeping, recovering bandit" picks which daily reminder to send and accounts for templates "recovering" novelty after rest. Deployed to millions of users, it raised total DAU by 0.5% and new-user retention by 2% over a strong baseline. — [Yancey & Settles, KDD 2020](https://research.duolingo.com/papers/yancey.kdd20.pdf)
- [Evidence, policy] Duolingo's rule is "protect the channel". The team can optimise timing, copy and localisation, but cannot raise notification volume without CEO approval. Groupon's up-to-5-emails-a-day example is cited as destroying a channel. — [Mazal, Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth)
- [Evidence, OLD 2021, peer-reviewed RCT] 17,500 retail-app users, 5 frequency levels over 7 weeks: as non-personalised push frequency rose, uninstalls rose and direct open rate fell. — [Wohllebe et al., Innovative Marketing 2021](https://businessperspectives.org/journals/innovative-marketing/issue-382/mobile-apps-in-retail-effect-of-push-notification-frequency-on-app-user-behavior)
- [Opinion/vendor] MoEngage says more notifications a day did not always raise uninstalls; no volumes given. — [MoEngage](https://moengage.com/?p=13056)
- [Evidence, vendor benchmarks, 2025-2026] Airship's median opt-in: Android 53.3% in 2025, down from 71.3% in 2023 and 59.5% in 2024; iOS about 48.85%. Batch: Android fell from 85% to 67% after Android 13 introduced the runtime permission. Pushwoosh: about 75% Android, about 56% iOS. The vendors measure differently, so the figures are not directly comparable. — [Airship 2026 benchmarks](https://www.airship.com/mobile-app-push-notification-benchmarks-for-2026/); [Batch](https://batch.com/blog/case-studies/benchmark-notifications-push-crm-mobile); [Pushwoosh](https://cdc.pushwoosh.com/blog/increase-push-notifications-opt-in)

**Home-screen widgets / lock screen / Live Activities**
- [Evidence, vendor case study, May 2026, correlational] Gratitude (a mindfulness app) put interactive journaling prompts, affirmations and metrics in Android widgets. Widget users showed 25% higher retention, and 10% of DAU adopted widgets after in-app prompts to add one. Widget users self-select, so this is not causal. — [Android Developers Blog, May 2026](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/05/how-gratitude-widgets-boosted-user-retention-25-percent.html?m=1)
- [Opinion] Duolingo's streak widget is credited with keeping the streak visible; no published effect size. — [Deconstructor of Fun teardown](https://duolingo.deconstructoroffun.com/mechanics/streaks)
- [Gap] No public effect data was found for iOS Live Activities or lock-screen countdowns.

**Daily content drops, community, personalisation**
- [Evidence, indirect] YouVersion's record days and plan subscriptions point to a daily content unit plus a collective calendar moment as the engine (see Q1). — [Webull/PR Newswire](https://www.webull.com/news/13969012967908352)
- [Evidence, indirect] Quran.com: only about 130K goals and 44K learning plans against 63M+ active users. Goal and plan features are niche among general users. — [Quran Foundation](https://quran.foundation/year-in-review/2025)
- [Evidence, qualitative] A 2023 PLOS Digital Health mixed-methods study of anxiety-app users found that users form their opinion of an app in the first days and leave many features unused. This matches Duolingo's "first 7 days" finding. — [Balaskas et al., PLOS Digital Health 2023](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10166273/1/journal.pdig.0000185.pdf)

### Inferences
- Five or more opens a day needs **naturally recurring, time-anchored reasons to look**. Gamification on top of a once-a-day content habit will not get there. For a Muslim app, the five salah times, sehri/iftar in Ramadan and the daily Hijri date are such anchors even for people who do not pray, as "what time is it in the religious day" information.
- Glanceable surfaces (a widget with the next prayer countdown, a lock-screen widget, a Ramadan Live Activity) probably *substitute for* some opens: the user sees the time without opening the app. The founder should track "daily touches" (widget impressions plus opens) and not just opens, or 5+ opens a day may be the wrong target.
- Notification lessons for this context:
  - Sending 5 adhan pushes a day to non-praying users risks the fatigue and uninstall pattern Wohllebe found. Make adhan pushes opt-in per prayer.
  - Default to one or two high-value pushes, for example a dua or verse tied to the time of day.
  - Copy Duolingo's "back off when ignored" rule.
- Streaks are worth testing only on a low-bar daily action (for example "read today's one ayah or dua"), with built-in grace days. The +0.38% freeze result suggests grace does not erode the habit. A prayer streak would penalise exactly the non-devout majority the founder is targeting.

### Gaps
- No primary Duolingo numbers on widget impact, the Streak Freeze churn effect, or optimal notification counts per day.
- No public studies on Live Activities engagement.
- No Sensor Tower or data.ai faith-app engagement reports were reachable (paywalled).
- No HCI study was found on streak-induced anxiety in Bible or Quran apps, though secondary summaries claim one exists for a Bible app.

## 3. Bangladesh context: habits, devices, data, content

### Takeaway
Bangladesh had about 82.8M internet users (47% penetration) in October 2025. Facebook ad reach was 64M and adult TikTok ad reach 56.2M, with TikTok growing fastest (+27% year on year). Users are heavily male (63-70%), almost entirely on Android, and mobile download speeds are moderate (36.8 Mbps median). DataReportal gives no time-spent or peak-hour data for Bangladesh. Local studies suggest young people spend 5-8 hours a day on social media, so the habit competition is Facebook and TikTok short video.

### Inferences
- The habit benchmark Bangladeshi users already have is Facebook and TikTok short video plus Messenger and WhatsApp chat, all checked many times a day. An Islamic app gets more frequent opens by being **shareable into those channels** (dua/verse cards for WhatsApp and Facebook) than by pulling users away from them.
- An audience that is about two-thirds male and Android-dominant on mid-range speeds favours a lightweight APK, Android widgets (not Live Activities, which are iOS-only) and low-data content (text and image cards over video by default).

### Gaps
- No Bangladesh-specific time-spent, sessions-per-day or hour-of-day peak data was found in DataReportal 2026 or elsewhere.
- No data on low-end device share (RAM/storage) or Bangladesh mobile data prices was found in this pass.
- No data on Islamic content (waz/lectures) consumption frequency on YouTube or Facebook in Bangladesh.

## 4. Islamic-ethics constraints on gamification (streaks, counters, leaderboards, riya, engagement farming)

### Takeaway
No formal fatwa or named-scholar ruling on worship streaks or leaderboards was found. Academic work on Islamic apps sees streaks and rewards as aids to consistency (istiqamah), but users object to ads during worship and to profit-driven design, and researchers call for design grounded in sincerity (ikhlas). The clearest riya risk is anything **public or comparative**: leaderboards and shared counts of worship. Private, self-directed tracking (closer to muhasaba, self-accounting) is much less contested.

### Cited Findings
- [Evidence, academic, 2025] A review of 11 popular Islamic lifestyle apps found that streak counters and reward systems are seen as supporting spiritual consistency, but some apps gave minimal feedback and fell short on intrinsic motivation. Participants objected to ads during worship. The authors call for apps grounded in "sincerity (ikhlas) and community service, rather than profit-driven models". — [Int. J. Human-Computer Interaction, 2025](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10447318.2025.2595545); [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/398791216_Islamic_Lifestyle_Applications_Meeting_the_Spiritual_Needs_of_Modern_Muslims)
- [Evidence, academic] Gamification (points, badges, leaderboards) significantly raised motivation in Islamic education classrooms and in digital Quran literacy. This is the education context, not personal worship. — [Adra Islamic Studies](https://research.adra.ac.id/index.php/islamicstudies/article/download/2411/1697); [ResearchGate, Quran literacy](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/396712685_The_Effectiveness_of_Gamification-Based_Islamic_Religious_Education_Learning_Models_on_Digital_Al-Qur'an_Literacy)
- [Opinion, Oct 2026] Op-ed on a Saudi Grand Mufti decree about filming prayers: "the structural mechanics of modern social media platforms are directly at odds with the cultivation of Ikhlas." — [Kashmir Reader](https://kashmirreader.com/2026/10/31/the-imperative-of-sincerity-ikhlas-vs-the-perils-of-public-display-riya/)
- [Opinion, 2026] Religious scholars criticised gamifying the Arbaeen pilgrimage, saying sacred narrative cannot be reduced to win/lose mechanics. — [report](https://vishveshwarinstitute.com/2026/08/05/https-khabaronline-ir-xqbhx-14-mrdad-1405-23-23-kd-mtlb-14-mrdad-1405-23-23-rway/)
- [Opinion, app blogs] Streaks and XP "reward showing up", and the niyyah stays the user's own. Others warn the gamification can become the goal. — [SABR blog](https://get-sabr.com/blog/best-apps-like-duolingo-for-quran-memorization); [Quranicma](https://quranicma.com/best-quran-memorization-apps-2026/)
- [Evidence, product] Quranly markets itself as a "habit-building Qur'an app". Some Quran apps run family and friend leaderboards. — [Quranly](https://www.quranly.app/); [Quranicma](https://quranicma.com/best-quran-memorization-apps-2026/)
- [Background] Muhasaba (self-accounting) is an established Islamic practice of private self-review, a doctrinal basis for private trackers. — [Wikipedia: Muhasaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhasaba)

### Inferences
- Lower-risk designs:
  - private trackers with no public sharing;
  - streaks framed as consistency (istiqamah), with forgiving grace;
  - no points or "rewards" attached to worship acts;
  - no ads on Quran or salah screens.
- Higher-risk designs:
  - public leaderboards of prayers or Quran pages;
  - shareable "I prayed X times" badges (riya);
  - loss-framed guilt pushes ("your streak will die") about worship;
  - dark-pattern engagement farming, which the 2025 paper's "profit-driven" critique targets.
- For non-devout users, a guilt-based worship streak may push them to uninstall, on top of the ethical issue. Gamify neutral learning actions (one dua a day, names of Allah quiz) rather than fard worship.

### Gaps
- No formal fatwa (Arabic, Bangla or English) on worship streaks, leaderboards or digital reward counters was found. Bangladeshi scholar or Islamic Foundation Bangladesh views are unknown. A targeted Arabic/Bangla search is needed.
- No user study measured riya perception or guilt from Islamic-app streaks.

## 5. Utility triggers that bring non-devout users back daily

### Takeaway
Prayer times are the second most-used Islamic digital resource (59.8%), after Quran apps (95.7%), with dua and dhikr third (53.9%). No source measures how often each utility (prayer-time check, Hijri date, Ramadan countdown, names, halal, zakat) is opened. The evidence supports prayer times and daily dua as the main recurring utilities. Names, halal and zakat look like episodic searches, not daily triggers, though this is not directly measured.

### Cited Findings
- [Evidence, self-reported, biased sample] Most-used Islamic digital resources: Quran apps 95.7%, prayer-time/Adhan 59.8%, Dua & Dhikr 53.9%, Islamic social media content 47.2%. Qibla, names, zakat and halal were not measured. — [GTAF State of the Ummah 1447](https://gtaf.org/state-of-the-ummah-1447/)
- [Evidence, same source] Self-reported Quran reading frequency: daily 57.6%, a few times a week 24%, only in Ramadan/special occasions 4%. This sample skews practising. — [GTAF](https://gtaf.org/state-of-the-ummah-1447/)
- [Evidence, small trial] Prayer-app trial (n=72): self-rated frequency of use 3.11 out of 5, interest in future use 4.31 out of 5. — [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05626673](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05626673)
- [Evidence, OLD] Malaysian diploma students (n=290) reported low frequency of digital Quran app use. — [HRMARS / UiTM](https://hrmars.com/papers_submitted/2664/The_Use_of_Digital_Quran_Application_among_Diploma_Students_of_UiTM_Perak_Branch,_Tapah_Campus.pdf)
- [Evidence, Ramadan] Quran apps see usage jumps in Ramadan. Bangladeshi media publish "top Quran apps for Ramadan 2025" lists, which suggests local seasonal demand. — [Religion News Service](https://religionnews.com/2020/05/20/tarteel-app-muslims-recite-memorize-learn-quran); [UNB](https://unb.com.bd/category/Tech/top-10-holy-quran-apps-on-ios-and-android-in-ramadan-2025/154765)
- [Evidence, analogue] YouVersion's top verses are comfort-in-hardship verses (anxiety, fear). Situational reassurance content is what users return to. — [ChurchLeaders](https://churchleaders.com/news/2209898-youversion-2025-verse-of-the-year-bobby-gruenewald.html)

### Inferences
- **Strongest daily or multi-daily trigger:** prayer and sehri/iftar times, shown at a glance in a widget or lock screen, with opt-in adhan.
- **Daily once:** a dua or ayah for the moment or situation (stress, exams, travel, illness), modelled on YouVersion's Verse of the Day. It is shareable to Facebook and WhatsApp, which fits Bangladeshi channel habits.
- **Calendar moments:** the Hijri date and the countdowns to Ramadan, Shab-e-Barat and Eid are seasonal spikes, like Hallow's Lent.
- **Episodic, not daily:** Islamic names, halal checks and zakat calculators are useful for acquisition and SEO, not for opens.
- Five distinct daily opens per user is not supported by any published faith-app data. A realistic stack is 1-2 notification-driven opens, plus glanceable widget touches at prayer times, plus spikes on special days.

### Gaps
- No per-feature usage frequency data from any Muslim app (prayer-time checks per day, Hijri views, dua opens).
- No evidence on how often non-praying Muslims check prayer times, for example to time their day, iftar or office breaks.
- No Bangladesh-specific Islamic-app usage data. GTAF's Bangladesh subsample reports no app-usage numbers.

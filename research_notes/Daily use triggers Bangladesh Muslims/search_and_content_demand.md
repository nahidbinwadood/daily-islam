# Search and Content Demand: What Bangladeshi Muslims Search for and Watch About Islam (2025–2026)

Research date: 4 November 2026. Primary data was pulled live on that date from three places: the Google Trends API (geo=BD), Google Autocomplete (gl=bd, hl=bn), and YouTube search results (gl=BD). Those numbers come from my own queries, not from a published report. Google Trends values are **relative indices**, not absolute search counts. I could not get absolute monthly search volumes because Google Ads Keyword Planner and Ahrefs/Semrush need logins.

**How to read the Trends index used below.** I ran 7 comparison sets of 5 terms each. Each set included "নামাজের সময়" (prayer time) as a shared anchor, so terms from different sets can be put on one scale: **anchor = 1.0, using the mean of the last 52 weeks (Nov 2025 – Nov 2026)**. Source for every Trends figure: Google Trends Explore, Bangladesh, past 5 years, weekly ([trends.google.com/trends/explore?geo=BD&date=today%205-y](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?geo=BD&date=today%205-y)), pulled via the Trends API on 2026-11-04. Caveat: Trends matches the exact phrase, so spelling variants are missed. For example, "সূরা ইয়াসিন" misses "ইয়াসিন সূরা", which Trends shows as the more common word order. Treat small terms as **floors**.

## Q1. What Islamic topics do Bangladeshis search most on Google (Bangla and Banglish)?

### Takeaway
On a normal (non-Ramadan) day, the largest Islamic search demand in Bangladesh is **dua for specific situations**, about 2.4x prayer-time searches. Next come **dream interpretation** (about 1.3x, and the fastest-growing Islamic query family, up roughly 2.5x since 2021) and **prayer times** (1.0x). After those, in order: Islamic baby names, ayat/surah lookups, waz, special-night dates, zakat, and tahajjud. Ramadan utilities (sehri/iftar time, niyat, iftar dua) are the biggest seasonal spikes. Many "what is X" queries look like student homework rather than practice.

- **Ranked demand themes**, ordered by daily-use potential and weighing Google volume, frequency, YouTube consumption and growth:
  1. **Prayer times / "what time is Fajr/Isha today" / forbidden times, by district.** Daily need with hourly peaks (Q3). On Google it is about 1.0 + 1.06 (Banglish) on the anchor scale. Probably the best "open several times a day" hook. Counterpoint: Google volume for prayer time is falling (2023 → 2026: −35%), likely because people moved to apps, widgets and in-SERP answers. Demand is not falling; it is moving off Google.
  2. **Situational dua with Bangla pronunciation and meaning** (namaz duas, qunut, masura, sleep, waking, toilet, travel, pain/illness, deceased, children, job, rizq, danger, earthquake, evil eye). This is the biggest head term (2.4x), and its long tail is driven by life situations.
  3. **Dream interpretation (স্বপ্নের ব্যাখ্যা).** About 1.3x prayer time and the only big term at an all-time high (2026). Searches happen right after waking (Q3). People who rarely pray still use it daily. Strong curiosity and engagement hook.
  4. **Ramadan utilities** (sehri/iftar time by district, niyat, iftar dua, tarawih rakat/dua, fitra amount, countdown to Ramadan/Eid). The single largest seasonal spike.
  5. **Amol/wazifa for problems and special days** (Shab-e-Barat amol, Friday amol, before-sleep amol, rizq, job, debt, accepted duas, becoming rich). Problem-driven and recurring.
  6. **Surah reading and listening** (Yasin, Rahman, Mulk, Kahf, Waqiah, Ayatul Kursi, the 3 Quls, last ayahs of Baqarah/Hashr), mostly as audio. Modest on Google but enormous on YouTube (Q2).
  7. **Waz and Q&A video** (Azhari, Ahmadullah). YouTube-native; Google interest in "ওয়াজ" is falling.
  8. **Islamic baby names with meaning, by first letter.** Steady but declining on Google.
  9. **Shareable Islamic status / caption / picture for Facebook.** Collapsing on Google in 2026 (index 9 in 2023 → 2), probably now generated with AI or sourced on social platforms.
  10. **Salah how-to and rakat counts** (নামাজ কয় রাকাত, ফরজ/ওয়াজিব, kaza namaz, wudu/ghusl fard). A learning need for non-regular practitioners.
  11. **Date lookups for special days** (Shab-e-Barat, Shab-e-Qadr, Shab-e-Meraj, Ashura, Eid "কবে", "কত দিন বাকি").
  12. **Death/janaza** (janaza niyat and dua, dua for the deceased, ghusl of the dead). Event-driven.
  13. **Zakat/fitra calculation and Qurbani masala.** Seasonal; users explicitly search "যাকাত ক্যালকুলেটর" and the official "ইসলামিক ফাউন্ডেশন" fitra figure.
  14. **Private and sensitive fiqh** (hayez, ghusl, intimacy, masturbation, talaq). Low head-term volume, but these are the most-read pages on IslamQA Bangla, and "sohobas" queries are rising. Users want private, trusted answers.
  15. **Ruqyah / evil eye / black magic / jinn.** Tiny on Google, big on YouTube audio.
  16. **Hajj/Umrah packages and costs.** Commercial intent.
  17. **Halal/haram, sud, business.** Mostly definitional queries and hadith quotes, not practical "is my bKash/bank halal" questions (Google shows little of the latter).
- **Bangla script beats Banglish** for dua (about 2:1). Banglish "namaz time" is falling faster than Bangla-script "নামাজের সময়". An app should be Bangla-first, with Banglish search tolerance.
- **Trust anchors appear inside queries.** "আল কাউসার" (Al-Kawsar), "ইসলামিক ফাউন্ডেশন" (Islamic Foundation), and "মিজানুর রহমান" are typed into queries. Showing the source and authority is a feature people want.
- **Searching on behalf of others** ("স্বামীর/সন্তানের/ছেলের চাকরির জন্য দোয়া") suggests mothers and wives are a core persona.
- Many queries ("কাকে বলে", "কত প্রকার", "শব্দের অর্থ") are school and madrasa homework. This is a real audience segment, but not a daily-practice one.

### Gaps
- No absolute monthly search volumes (Keyword Planner, Ahrefs and Semrush need logins). All volumes here are relative.
- Could not retrieve "most popular" lists from ihadis, hadithbd, muslimbangla, alkawsar or islamhouse bn in the time available. Only IslamQA bn exposes view counts.
- Google Trends exact-phrase matching undercounts terms with many spellings (দোয়া/দুআ/দোআ; সূরা/সুরা; নামাজ/নামায).

## Q3. What are the seasonal and daily peaks?

### Takeaway
**Ramadan week 1 is the year's biggest peak** for nearly every Islamic term. **Shab-e-Barat week** produces the single highest spike of any religious term. **Friday** multiplies Surah Kahf and Jumu'ah searches about 30–40x and lifts dua searches about 12%. Within a day, prayer-time searches peak **04:00–06:00 (before and at Fajr)** with a second peak at **15:00–17:00 (Asr–Maghrib)**. Dua searches peak at 05:00–06:00, and **dream-interpretation searches peak 05:00–08:00, straight after waking**.

### Cited Findings (all Google Trends BD, pulled 2026-11-04 — [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?geo=BD))
- **Ramadan:** the 5-year peak week for prayer time, dua, sehri end time, sehri time, iftar time, and dua (Latin) was **19–25 Mar 2023** (start of Ramadan 1444). ### Inferences
- **Daily triggers:** (1) pre-Fajr and Fajr wake-up, with prayer time, morning dua and the night's dream all clustered in 04:00–08:00; (2) Asr–Maghrib, with prayer time and evening adhkar; (3) bedtime, with sleep dua, Mulk and Ayatul Kursi audio (from YouTube consumption; Trends hourly did not isolate a bedtime spike for "দোয়া"). A morning "dream + dua + today's times" card matches the strongest observed daily peak.
- **Weekly trigger:** Friday (Kahf, Jumu'ah niyat/rakat/khutbah, Friday amol, Asr-on-Friday dua). Also Thursday night.
- **Annual calendar, in descending spike size:** Shab-e-Barat → Ramadan week 1 (sehri/iftar/niyat/dua/tarawih) → Laylatul Qadr → Eid-ul-Fitr (Eid prayer method, fitra amount) → Dhul Hijjah/Arafah → Eid-ul-Adha (Qurbani date, masala) → Ashura → Shab-e-Meraj. "কত দিন বাকি" countdown searches appear for Ramadan, Eid and Qurbani all year.
- Disaster and death news creates sudden dua demand (earthquake, janaza). Push-ready situational dua content can be timed to news.

### Gaps
- Hourly data covers one week in November (no Ramadan). Ramadan hourly patterns (sehri 02:00–04:00 and pre-iftar) are inferred, not measured.
- No published Bangladesh-specific app-usage seasonality (Sensor Tower, data.ai, Adjust) was found.

## Q4. What life events trigger Islamic searches?

### Takeaway
Life events do generate distinct, recurring Islamic search clusters, and Google Autocomplete confirms each one: **birth** (names by letter with meaning; aqiqah rules and dua), **marriage** (khutbah, dua, intimacy rulings), **death** (janaza niyat/dua, dua for the deceased, ghusl of the deceased, status posts), **illness/pain** (dua for the sick, pain dua, shifa), **travel**, **exams/results**, **job hunting** (often searched for a husband or son), **debt and rizq**, **new house or car**, **Hajj/Umrah** (packages and costs), and **divorce** (talaq rules, notice forms, talaq given in anger).

### Cited Findings (Google Autocomplete BD, 2026-11-04 — [autocomplete endpoint](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=bn&gl=bd&q=%E0%A6%86%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%BE); Google Trends related queries — [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?geo=BD&date=today%2012-m))
- **Birth:** আকিকা → দেওয়ার নিয়ম, আকিকার দোয়া, "আকিকার দোয়া মেয়ে সন্তানের", "আকিকা না দিলে কি হয়" (what if aqiqah isn't done), "আকিকার মাংস বন্টনের নিয়ম মিজানুর রহমান" (meat distribution, named scholar). Names: "ইসলামিক নাম" is the #1 "ইসলামিক" query; "ছেলেদের ইসলামিক নাম অর্থসহ ২০২৬" is a Breakout. Trends: "সন্তানের জন্য দোয়া" (10), "sontan laver dua" (+60%), "গর্ভাবস্থায় সহবাস করার ইসলামিক নিয়ম" (+350%).
- **Marriage:** বিয়ের → বিয়ের খুতবা, বিয়ের দোয়া (mixed with non-religious: cards, saree, mehendi). Rising "sohobas korar dua" (+250%). Dream queries about marriage are #3 ("স্বপ্নে বিয়ে দেখলে কি হয়", 73).
- **Divorce:** তালাক → দেওয়ার নিয়ম, তালাকের নোটিশ, তালাক নামা / ফরম ডাউনলোড, তালাকের মাসআলা. IslamQA bn's #5 most-read is talaq given in anger (70,781 views). — [islamqa.info/bn](https://islamqa.info/bn)
- **Death:** মৃত ব্যক্তির → জন্য দোয়া (Bangla/Arabic/hadith), জন্য স্ট্যাটাস, গোসলের নিয়ম, জন্য করণীয়. জানাজার → নামাজের দোয়া, নিয়ত, নিয়ম, "দোয়া সমূহ pdf". Trends: জানাজার দোয়া (21) and জানাজার নামাজের দোয়া (17) rank among the top dua queries; "জানাজার নামাজের নিয়ম ও দোয়া" is rising +60%. Dreams of graves and dead people are common (26, 23).
- **Illness:** অসুস্থ → অসুস্থ ব্যক্তির জন্য দোয়া, "অসুস্থ নিয়ে স্ট্যাটাস দোয়া", "অসুস্থতা নিয়ে ইসলামিক স্ট্যাটাস". Trends: ব্যথার দোয়া (14), সুস্থতার জন্য দোয়া (8); English "dua for pain / pain relief / shifa".
- **Travel:** সফরের দোয়া → বাংলা, আরবি, "আল কাউসার", অর্থ সহ, Banglish "soforer dua", নিরাপদ সফরের দোয়া; English "dua for traveling" is the #1 "dua for" completion.
- **Exams:** পরীক্ষার → "পরীক্ষার রেজাল্ট ভালো হওয়ার দোয়া" (dua for good results), alongside routine and result queries; English "dua for exam".
- **Job:** চাকরির জন্য দোয়া → স্বামীর, সন্তানের, ছেলের, ভালো, হালাল, নতুন. Trends: "চাকরি পাওয়ার আমল" rising +80%.
- **Rizq and debt:** রিজিক বৃদ্ধির দোয়া ও আমল, "ঋণ মুক্তির দোয়া ও আমল" (+40%), "ধনী হওয়ার আমল", "হালাল ভাবে ধনী হওয়ার দোয়া".
- **Danger and anxiety:** বিপদ থেকে মুক্তির দোয়া, বিপদে পড়লে কোন দোয়া; YouTube "দুশ্চিন্তা ও অস্থিরতা দূর করার দোয়া" — [video](https://youtu.be/viGs6NXJUjA).
- **New house:** নতুন ঘরে উঠার/প্রবেশের দোয়া, নতুন ঘর উদ্বোধনের দোয়া, "নতুন ঘর বন্ধ করার নিয়ম" (ritual "closing" of a new house), plus dream queries about a new house.
- **New car:** "নতুন গাড়ি কিনলে দোয়া" appears in autocomplete, but it is minor next to non-religious car queries.
- **Hajj/Umrah:** ওমরাহ → করার নিয়ম, প্যাকেজ 2026 বাংলাদেশ মূল্য, ভিসা ফি, খরচ; হজ → হজ্জ নিবন্ধন 2027 শেষ তারিখ (registration deadline), হজ্জ প্যাকেজ ২০২৭, হজের ফরজ কয়টি.
- **Evil eye / jinn / magic (perceived affliction):** "bod nojor er dua" +90%; ruqyah audio with millions of views (Q2); "রুকইয়াহ সেন্টার ঢাকা" and "রুকইয়াহ সাপোর্ট বিডি" appear in autocomplete.
- **Women's purity issues:** হায়েজ অবস্থায় কুরআন পড়ার বিধান; "মোবাইলে কুরআন পড়া যাবে কি"; IslamQA bn's #1 most-read is the semen/madhi/discharge distinction (223,616 views). — [islamqa.info/bn](https://islamqa.info/bn)

### Inferences
- The best life-event features look like **small, searchable "situation packs"**: the dua with Bangla pronunciation and meaning, a 1-line ruling, and a source. Examples: new baby (names + aqiqah), death in the family (janaza steps + dua for the deceased + condolence status), sick relative, exam/result day, job interview, travel, new home.
- **Proxy and family use is clear** (job dua for a husband or son, dua for children, aqiqah for a daughter). Features that let a user make dua or amol "for" someone fit this behaviour.
- **Shareable output** ("…স্ট্যাটাস", "…ক্যাপশন" attached to illness, death and Ramadan) is a recurring need. Making a shareable Facebook card for life events is cheap and matches observed demand.
- Sensitive topics (intimacy, purity, talaq) are high-read but low-search-volume, likely because of embarrassment. An anonymous, private Q&A feature would serve a real gap.

### Gaps
- Car and house-purchase triggers are weak on Google, so they may not justify dedicated features.
- No quantitative split of life-event search volume by gender or age.
- No data on how many searchers act on results (e.g., actually performing aqiqah); only information demand is shown.
- No direct evidence on bank/bKash/insurance halal-haram queries beyond definitional "সুদ" searches. Practical Islamic-finance questions may live on Facebook groups and YouTube Q&A rather than Google.
